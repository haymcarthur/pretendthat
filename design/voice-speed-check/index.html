Nutmeg speed check

Same recording, played at different speeds. Speed and pitch go up together, the way the app does it today. Which one sounds like Nutmeg "at 1.2" to you?

How to use it. Play down the list for line 1. Note the number that sounds like the Nutmeg you picked. Do the same for line 2. Then tell Claude the two numbers.

Want to check without seeing the numbers? Tap Blind mode. The rows get shuffled and labeled with letters. Pick a letter, then tap Reveal.

If you can, also listen on the iPad, where you usually hear her. The iPad's small speaker makes her sound higher and thinner than laptop speakers or headphones do.
