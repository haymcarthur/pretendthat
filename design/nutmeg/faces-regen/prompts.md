# New Nutmeg faces for the one-scene play (2026-11-21)

Attach `design/nutmeg/nutmeg-faces-v2.webp` (the current face sheet) to both. Generate in ChatGPT, vectorize in Figma, and export each as one SVG the same size and framing as `design/export/nutmeg/nutmeg-face-ooh.svg`. `tools/import_art.py` splits eyes and mouth into `nutmeg-face-<name>` + `nutmeg-face-<name>-mouth`. The app uses them as soon as the assets exist (it falls back to ooh/happy until then).

## 1. Alarmed ("uh-oh!"): for tense events
Using the attached mouse character's face sheet as the exact style guide (same head shape, brown fur, big pink ears, cream muzzle, eye style, line weight, flat vector style), draw ONE new expression of her face, front view, same size and framing as the faces on the sheet: alarmed but playful, like she just spotted a problem and is about to call for help. Eyes wide open with small pupils, eyebrows raised and tilted up in the middle (worried), ears perked up, mouth a small open "oh no" shape (an oval, slightly wavy). Kid-friendly, not scary. No hands, no body, no headset changes. Solid white background, nothing else in the image.

## 2. Phew (relieved): for the calm-again lines
Same attached face sheet as the style guide. Draw ONE new expression of her face, front view, same size and framing: relieved, the "phew, that was close" moment.
