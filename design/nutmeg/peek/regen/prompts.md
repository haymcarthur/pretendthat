# Peek art regeneration prompts (attach reference-current-peek.png to both)

## 1. Body, no head, no hands (attach reference-headless-body.png AND reference-current-peek.png)
Using the attached headless mouse body as the style guide (same brown fur, purple vest with a yellow star, cream chest, flat vector style), draw a NEW pose of the same body with NO head: the neck ends in a clean flat cut at the top, like the reference. She is leaning on a table edge and peeking over it, like the second reference: shoulders forward, elbows bent and out to the sides, forearms coming forward and resting along the table edge in front of her chest. End each forearm at the wrist with a clean flat cut. NO hands or paws. Everything below the table line is hidden, so the image ends in a straight horizontal line just below her forearms. Include her tail curling up on the left. Solid white background, nothing else in the image.

## 2. Paws only
Using the attached mouse character's paws exactly (same brown fur, darker brown finger lines, same flat vector style), draw ONLY her two paws, side by side with space between them, curled over the top edge of a table as if gripping it, seen from the front. Show each paw from the wrist down; the wrist ends in a straight horizontal cut at the bottom. No arms, no body, no table drawn. Solid flat green background #00C853, nothing else in the image.
