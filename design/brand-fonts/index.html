PretendThat brand font sampler

Five heading fonts Haylee picked for the grown-up-facing side (site, onboarding, Grown-ups section): Momo Trust Display, Cal Sans, Tilt Warp, Poppins, Lexend. Kid screens keep SF Rounded. Same copy, same brand colors, same chunky buttons. Only the fonts change. Start in blind mode, pick the letter you like best by eye, then reveal. Click a card to open its full sample below.

Letters are shuffled on each load. "Today" (Fredoka + Inter) is a labeled baseline, not a candidate.

All five load from Google Fonts and are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (free to embed in the app and use on the web). Momo Trust Display, Cal Sans and Tilt Warp have a single weight, so their body text uses a separate family (named on each card when you reveal).
