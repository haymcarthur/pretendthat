Do her recorded lines match her live voice now?

Each sound is one of Nutmeg's recorded lines (a clip), a short pause, then a live line, the way they meet in a game.

How to listen. For each line, play Before, then After. The live line is the same in both. Listen for the jump where the clip ends and the live line starts. In After, both halves should sound like one mouse.

What changed. Before, each clip had its own pitch move (up to 5 semitones) plus a vowel adjustment, so the clips sounded processed next to her live voice. Now clips get exactly what live lines get (+2, nothing else), and all 13 built-in lines were re-recorded so each one sits in her normal live range.

All sounds went through the app's own code (pitch shifter, then speed 1.20), rendered on the Mac. The last sound was recorded from the app on the simulator.
