Screen 1 · iPad · 2 kids · setup

One checklist per role, Nutmeg on a card

The scene title (with its 🔊) sits top-left; the two role cards share the stage. Each item: checkbox left, 🔊 right (44 pt or more). Pilot's "Set up the control panel" is being read (filled 🔊, solid outline); Engineer's "Hang the sign" was tapped while she talked, so it's queued (dashed NEXT). Nutmeg peeks over the top edge of the right-hand card, paws on it, focus eyes on the rows. ◀ Previous scene and ▶ Next scene are one centered pair, same size and shape.

Screen 2 · iPad · 6 kids · setup

Two rows of three, Nutmeg on the top-right card

Cards shrink to 290 pt so the grid clears the right curtain (picture 46, text 17, rows 52 pt, checkbox and 🔊 still 44 pt). Nutmeg sits on the top-right card (Mission Control): the only top-row card with no title above it, and her head fits under the valance. The centered button pair sits under the grid. Alien's card is finished: its header shows the green check.

Screen 3 · iPad · scenario moment

Every box ticked: Nutmeg takes center stage

The cards fold into a strip of role pictures with green checks ("All done!"). Nutmeg hops off the card and comes out whole and big on the floor, saying the short "Pretend that…" line, shown in a speech card with the hear-again button. The same centered button pair; ▶ Next scene gets a soft halo.

Screen 4 · iPhone · 2 kids · setup

Setup: the checklists live in a shelf

Like the chat shelf (round 40): edge to edge, attached to the bottom, top corners rounded, white, starting right under the top bar. The header holds Nutmeg's live head, face-cropped in a circle (as in the chat shelf), the scene title and its 🔊. No peek, so the shelf is taller and the rows get more air. The role cards (pinned-card style: white with a hairline) fill the shelf; two fit without scrolling. The Prev / Next pair is pinned to the shelf's bottom.

Screen 5 · iPhone · 6 kids (scrolled)

6 kids: the cards scroll inside the shelf

The shelf header (her live head, the title) and the buttons stay put; only the card list scrolls (scroll bar, soft fades at both ends, a cut-off card). Here it's scrolled to Mission Control / Astronaut.

Screen 6 · iPhone · scenario

Scenario: the shelf slides away

Transition: when the last box is ticked the shelf slides down off the screen and Nutmeg grows from the header circle to her whole body on the stage (one stageMove spring). The line card with 🔊 sits under her, the roles collapse into a done strip, and the same button pair glows. ▶ Next scene: the shelf slides back up with the next scene's cards and she shrinks back into the header circle.
