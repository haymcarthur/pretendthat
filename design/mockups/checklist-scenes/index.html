Play scenes: checklists, then the scenario

Static mockup of the 2026-11-20 design · iPad 1180 × 820 pt landscape, iPhone 393 × 852 pt portrait · example game "Rocket to the Moon" · not built

Screen 1 · iPad · 2 kids · setup

One checklist per role, side by side

The scene title (with its 🔊) sits on top; the two role cards share the stage. Each item has a checkbox on the left and a 🔊 on the right (44 pt or more). Pilot's "Set up the control panel" is being read (filled 🔊, a solid outline in the role color); Engineer's "Hang the sign" was tapped while she talked, so it's queued (dashed). Nutmeg peeks over the top-right corner of the card area with her focus eyes on the rows. ◀ Previous scene is a white secondary pill beside the green ▶ Next scene.

Screen 2 · iPad · 6 kids · setup

Two rows of three, same corner for Nutmeg

The cards get the denser size (picture 48, text 18, rows 52 pt, checkbox and 🔊 still 44 pt). Nutmeg's peek fits in the top-right corner at a smaller size, on the top edge of the third card, under the valance. The ▶ / ◀ buttons move into the bottom-right corner below the grid. Alien's card is finished: its header shows the green check.

Screen 3 · iPad · scenario moment

Every box ticked: Nutmeg takes center stage

The cards fold into a strip of role pictures with green checks ("All done!"). Nutmeg comes out whole and big, standing on the floor in the middle, and says the short "Pretend that…" line, shown beside her in a speech card with the hear-again button (the control bar's icon). The ▶ Next scene button gets a soft halo because now it's time to act it out and move on.

Alternate A · iPad · 6 kids · Nutmeg bottom-left

Option: she peeks up over the stage edge, under the rail

Instead of the top-right corner, Nutmeg peeks up from below the front of the stage in the empty corner under the step rail, like a prompter. She never sits on a card or near the valance, but the corner under the rail is short (the rail ends at 655 pt), so she stays small (about 110 pt), she's far from the cards on the right, and she looks up instead of down at the rows.

Screen 4 · iPhone · 2 kids

Cards stacked, Nutmeg on the top edge

The title wraps to two lines on the left; Nutmeg peeks over the top edge of the card area on the right (the same corner as the iPad). Two cards fit without scrolling. ◀ is a 56 pt circle, ▶ Next scene the green pill.

Screen 5 · iPhone · 6 kids (scrolled)

The list scrolls under her paws

Nutmeg is pinned to the top edge of the scroll area; the cards slide under her paws. Here it's scrolled to Mission Control / Astronaut; the scroll bar and the cut-off card show there's more. Title and buttons stay put.

Screen 6 · iPhone · scenario

Big Nutmeg, short line, done strip

The stage grows: Nutmeg fills the top, the line card with 🔊 sits under her, the finished roles collapse into a strip, and ▶ Next scene glows.

Alternate B · iPhone · 6 kids

Option: she peeks up from the bottom bar

Nutmeg sits in the bottom-left corner beside ◀ / ▶, looking up; the cards scroll behind her head. The title gets the full width (one line), but her head covers the start of the rows as they scroll past, so this costs more than it saves.
