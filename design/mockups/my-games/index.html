3

Bookshelf, 3 games

Tapping My games opens Haylee's bookshelf on the same stage, with Nutmeg pointing at it. Each saved game is a picture card standing on a shelf (sign art, or the sticker card for no-printer games), newest first, three per shelf, with its name on a label on the shelf's edge. Empty shelves show there's room for more. The back arrow (top left) goes back to the start screen. Tap a card to replay it.
