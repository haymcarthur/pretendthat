Nutmeg pitch test 2

Ways to get the +2 you liked without the "talking inside a tunnel" sound.

How to use it. Play "This is Nutmeg" first. Then, for each line, play down the list. For each version ask two things: does she sound like she's in a tunnel (hollow, far away, phasey)? And is she too fast? Note the one you'd ship.

Blind mode shuffles each line's versions under letters A to J. Pick a letter, then tap Reveal.

The first two rows are the anchors: Today and the Old +2 (the tunnel one). Every version is level-matched, so louder never wins. If you can, also try the iPad speaker.

What I measured

What the tunnel sound is. In a real voice, every vocal-cord pulse lines all the harmonics up at once, so the wave has one sharp click per cycle. Phase-vocoder shifters, like Apple's TimePitch, rebuild each harmonic on its own and the clicks smear out. The ear hears that as hollow and far away. I measured it as pulse sharpness: how peaky those clicks are, compared with natural speech at the same pitch (100% = natural).

  1. Speed only (1.35 and 1.27): 100%. No processing, so no tunnel. The only cost is that she talks faster.
  2. PSOLA (+2, and the split): about 90%. It copies her real pulses, so most of the sharpness survives. The trade-off is a little roughness or hoarseness: more noise between harmonics than natural, and the harmonics are a little less clean. The split only shifts +1, so it has less of this.
  3. Signalsmith (every setting) and TimePitch: 73 to 76%. Both are phase-vocoder style, and both lose the same amount. That's true even at +1: the split with TimePitch or Signalsmith +1 measures the same 75%. So I don't expect Signalsmith to remove the tunnel. Its "vowels kept" setting does avoid the chipmunk shift, which may still sound nicer.

Smearing in time (echo before a word starts and a tail after it ends) is small for all of them, within about 1 dB. Signalsmith's default 120 ms block added 2 to 4 dB with "vowels kept". That's why this page uses a shorter 60 ms block for Signalsmith.

My ranking for the least tunnel at about +2: 1. Speed 1.35. 2. Split 1.27 + PSOLA +1. 3. PSOLA +2. 4. Signalsmith +2 with vowels kept. 5. Signalsmith +2. 6. Old TimePitch +2. Signalsmith +3 measures like +2, only higher. Speed 1.27 is clean, but it's only +1. I can't listen, so these are measurements. Your ear decides.
