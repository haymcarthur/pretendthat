Nutmeg pitch test

Her live voice, raised in pitch without changing her speed, next to the greeting clip you said sounds right.

How to use it. Play "This is Nutmeg" first. Then, for each line, play down the list and find the one that sounds most like her. Note the step (for example "+5"). It's fine if your pick isn't the "closest" tag: that tag only means the middle of her pitch matches the clip.

Blind mode shuffles each line's versions and labels them with letters. Pick a letter, then tap Reveal.

Listen for her sounding buzzy, metallic or "auto-tuned", not just for how high she is. If you can, also try it on the iPad speaker.

What I measured

Where the match is. Her live lines sit 5.7 to 7.8 semitones below the greeting clip (raw 249–280 Hz vs 389 Hz). One fixed raise of about +6 to +7 matches the clip on average. Per line, the closest step was +8, +6, +7 and +6.

Artifacts. Up to +3 the raised voice measures almost like plain natural speech at that pitch. From +4 it gets cleaner than natural: the breathy noise between her harmonics drops by about 30% at +4/+5 and about 40–45% at +6 to +8. That is the usual sign of a buzzy, slightly metallic, "auto-tuned" edge. I can't hear, so this is a measurement, not a listen. Please judge it by ear. Her timing doesn't change (same length, no added delay).

Something to know. The higher steps also move her vowel sounds up (the "chipmunk" effect). At +6 on top of today's 1.20 she is at about 1.7× her natural voice in total, which is a lot. If +4 or +5 already sounds like the clip to you, that's the safer pick.

All files are level-matched (within 1 dB) so louder never wins.
