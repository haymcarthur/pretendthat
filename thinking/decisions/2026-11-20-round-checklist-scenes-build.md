## 1. Guide and scene model (NutmegCore)
- **The one guide call** (`PhaseService.earlyGuide`, `KitScenesSix.checklistSchema`, `Prompts.checklistScenes`) writes the story, **4 play scenes** and the clean-up title. A scene is `{title, scenario, R1…R6: [items]}`: a 2–5 word title, 2–3 items per ranked role (2–6 words, one kid-doable action) and ONE "Pretend that…" scenario that always ends with an action for everyone. No setup scene, no goals, no page jobs, and no spoken task texts.
- **What Nutmeg says for an item is written by the app** ("Captain, put on your badge!", `ChecklistScenes.say`), so it costs no output tokens and replays say the same words. (Claude's call.)
- **Storage:** the guide's scenes keep `Scene.checklist = true`; each step is one item (shown text in `short`, role in `role`). `Kit.isChecklist` tells the paths apart. Older kits never have the flag.
- **Scenes by count** (`ChecklistScenes.visible`): 1–2 kids see 4, 3–4 see scenes 1, 2 and 4, 5–6 see 1 and 4. Dropping scene 3 (not 2) at 3–4 kids is Claude's call: the scenarios build towards the last scene, and scene 2 is usually the first twist. Only the playing roles' cards show; a count change needs no AI call.
- **Setup folded in, every item used** (`ChecklistScenes.cover`, `ChecklistCoverage`): the prompt asks for "Put on your badge" for every role in scene 1, the other pages spread over the scenes, and every page in scene 1 or scene 4 too. Each prop's weave rewrites 1–2 checklist items ("Set up the airplane seats"; `GuideWeave.checklistPrompt`). Then, **per count, when the scenes are composed**, any page or found prop the playing kids wouldn't see is added by the app: badges as "Put on your badge" for every playing role in the first scene; anything else as one item ("Hang up the sign", "Set up the snack cart") for the role it suits if it has room, else the least busy role. Deterministic, no AI call. Tested at every count 1–6 with found, swapped and skipped props, and with none.
- **Repair** (`ChecklistScenes.repair`, through `GuideRepair.repair`): real titles, exactly 4 scenes (template scenes from the roles' jobs), 1–3 clean items per role (a role with none gets two from its job; more than 3 are cut), no quotes or role titles in items, a run-on item cut to its first clause, scenario fixed ("Pretend that" + an everyone ending), only real objects (`realProps`). Idempotent. **A role with just one item is left alone** (Claude's call: padding it would be busywork).
- **Clean-up** stays a final checklist (`ChecklistScenes.cleanupScene`): every found prop goes back (the AI's clean-up job from the weave, else the app's "Put the bowl away"); roles with room tidy a page (at most 3 items each, badges last); a role with nothing gets "Give high fives". No scenario. When every clean-up item is checked, The End follows by itself after 1.2 s.
- **No re-ask calls for checklist kits** (Claude's call, cost): page clean-up jobs are the app's own, prop clean-up jobs come with the weave, anything missing is app-written.
- **Saved games:** schema 6. Schemas 2–5 still load and replay on their old path (no `checklist` flag → ensemble tasks / setup / goal rows exactly as before). Tests load each of 2, 3, 4, 5 and replay them, and round-trip a schema 6 game (same words on replay).
- **Bundled game** (`airplane-checklists.json`, `Fallback.checklistAirplane`): the airplane game as 4 checklist scenes. The store's fallback and demo mode use it (`AdventureStore.useChecklistFallback`, default on), so demo screenshots show the new format with no AI. `Fallback.airplane` keeps the round 36 shape for the older-path tests.

### Cost (estimate; no live call was made)
- **Guide:** round 40's guide measured ≈ 7,460 in / 2,430 out ≈ 7.6–7.9¢. The new answer has about 4 × (title + scenario + ~15 items of ~6 tokens + keys) + the story ≈ **800–1,000 output tokens** (the per-task `say` texts, setup/clean-up goals and page jobs are gone), and the prompt + schema are about 10% shorter (≈ 6,700 in). Estimate ≈ 2.7¢ in + 1.8¢ out ≈ **4.5¢, about −3¢ per new game**.
- **Weave per prop:** about the same input (short items instead of long tasks), shorter output (no setup job): ≈ −0.2¢ each. The job re-ask (~2¢ when it happened) no longer runs for checklist kits.
- **Voice (Realtime):** the scene opening is now a short cue (≈ 60–80 audio tokens instead of 135–171) plus the scenario once per scene (≈ 100). Item reads are short (≈ 30–40 tokens) but there are more of them; each is said live once and then plays from the same-game take / saved clip. Roughly the same per game; replays ≈ 0.

## 2. Play flow (app)
- **Cue on open** (`ChecklistScenes.cue`, `AdventureStore.sceneLine`): "Scene 2: Get the Plane Ready! Tap your picture to hear your jobs." / "Clean-up time: Back at the Gate! …". App-written, so it goes through `speak` and is recorded/replayed like every app line (D3b clips, same-game takes). Going back to a finished scene says its scenario again.
- **Cards** (`PretendThat/Screens/ChecklistSceneView.swift`): per playing role, picture + name + "1 of 3" (a ✓ when done); each item has the round check on the LEFT and a 🔊 on the RIGHT (the item text reads it too). Tapping the picture/name reads the role's whole checklist in one line (`ChecklistScenes.roleLine`; the voice `read_job` does the same).
- **Taps queue** (`ChecklistReader` in NutmegCore, `AppModel.readChecklist` / `runChecklistReader`): a tap while she talks waits its turn, no visible marker; only the row being read is highlighted. Row taps no longer stop her (the old `readTask` did), so lines never overlap. The title 🔊 joins the same queue.
- **Scenario moment:** the last check → she says the scenario (after the row she's reading; queued rows are dropped), the cards fold into the "All done!" strip with each role's picture, and the stage Nutmeg comes back in the normal stage framing (`.propCard`, the camera's existing preset; the mockup's drawn stage is ignored). "Hear it again" re-reads it.
- **◀ Previous / ▶ Next** (`PTSceneNavPair`): one centered pair at the bottom, same size and shape, Next green, Previous white. On the first checklist scene Previous isn't shown (Claude's call: hidden, so Next sits alone in the middle). Previous never goes back into the story and keeps the checks. A count change clears the checks.
- **Layout:** iPad = cards in a row (1–3 per row; 4 = 2 × 2; 5–6 = 3 + 2/3), Nutmeg's peek (body behind, paws in front) on the top-right card, rising beside the title pill; three density tiers, and the board scrolls if it ever doesn't fit. iPhone = a shelf with rounded top corners holding all the cards (scrolls inside), her live head (`PTNutmegAvatar`) in a circle beside the title, 14 pt between items. Checks and 🔊 are 44–56 pt. Chat open: the panel's Scene tab shows the title, compact cards (or the scenario) and the pair.
- **Voice tools:** `previous_scene` added (same pattern as `next_scene`), `read_job` reads the role's checklist, the session instructions and the play note describe checklists + scenario. The tools array changed, so the session's cached prefix is rebuilt once.

## 3. Skip and resume
- **Skip (replays only):** on a replay's Props and Print, a round ⏭ icon button (`PTBigIconButton(kind: .skip)`, bottom right) goes straight to the scenes; the saved props count as found. **Nutmeg says nothing about it** (Claude's call: a new line would need a new recording; the button is an extra for grown-ups who already did the hunt). A new game never shows it.
- **Resume:** while a scene is on screen, `ResumePoint` (saved game id, count, scene index, checks, time; nothing about a kid) is kept in UserDefaults on this device. It's cleared at The End and when the family goes home on purpose (X / Home). After a relaunch, the start screen shows the game's picture with a ▶ (the saved-game offer's style, top left; a small ✕ forgets it) for up to 3 days (Claude's call). A tap opens the saved game as a replay straight at that scene with its checks (`AdventureStore.resumeReplay`); its cue is the only line. A new game can be resumed only once it has auto-saved (at Print). Resume skips the pass check (Claude's call: it continues the game the family was already playing). Known limit: if the first play skipped a prop, the resumed replay has it as found, so the coverage step may add or move an item and a check can land on a neighbouring row.

## 4. Thinner button edges
- `Theme.edge(height)` = about 4% of the height, rounded to half a point, 2–4 pt; presses travel the same. Used by PTBigIconButton, the Next/Previous pair, the start and Saved games buttons, the onboarding button, the saved-game page buttons, the shelf arrows, the grown-up number keys, the round checks, the 🔊 row buttons and the toggle bubble. `Theme.depth` is now 4 (the most a button has; layout room under buttons) and `Theme.cardDepth` 3 (cards, rows, tiles).

## Review pass (2026-11-20, Claude, no Xcode: a "be the compiler" read of every changed app file)
Every identifier, initializer, enum case and memberwise init the new app code calls was looked up against its definition (NutmegCore and app); exhaustive switches over the cases that grew (`PhoneContent.scenario`, `PTBigIconButton.Kind.skip`, `UserAction.previousScene/.skipToScenes`, `ToolCall.previousScene`) are covered; iOS 17 APIs used (`Text.foregroundStyle` in a `Text + Text`, `UnevenRoundedRectangle(topLeadingRadius:topTrailingRadius:style:)`, `scrollIndicatorsFlash(onAppear:)`, `sensoryFeedback(_:trigger:condition:)`, `ViewThatFits`) have the signatures used. project.yml globs `PretendThat/`, so the new file is picked up by xcodegen. Nothing in NutmegCore changed in this pass.

**Fixed:**
- `AppModel.runChecklistReader` (the 🔊 queue): the wait for her line to end was unbounded (`while isSpeaking`), so a speaking flag that never cleared would have held every later tap for good. Now capped at 30 s, and it also waits while a live line is on its way or running (`realtime.voiceBusy`), so a slow live line (over the 4 s start wait) no longer gets the next row sent on top of it. `readChecklist` uses the same busy test.
- `AppModel.resumeSession`: since a resume may now run while a silent reconnect is running, it cleared the reconnect's `resuming` flag after its ping, which let a second reconnect start beside the first. It now only sets/clears the flag when it owns it.
- `ChecklistSceneView.PTChecklistBoard`: the body (layout math + nested ForEach + peek overlays in one expression) was split into a `BoardLayout` (computed once, `@MainActor` because `NutmegPeek`'s statics are View-isolated) and small `board` / `titleRow` / `cardRow` / `cardView` functions, to stay clear of "unable to type-check in reasonable time".

**Still uncertain (only a Mac build / device game settles these):**
- No real type-check yet: Swift 6 strict-concurrency diagnostics in RealtimeSession (Tasks created in MainActor methods, `os.Logger` as a global `let`) and SwiftUI overload resolution were checked by reading, not compiled.
- Watchdog false positive: any app line with no audio in 4 s closes the main socket (silent reconnect, line said again). A legitimately slow first delta would cost a reconnect; at most one per 20 s.
- The lines-session reopen count (2, 10, 30 s) is per connection and is not reset by a successful reopen.
- `voiceBusy` now includes `lineInFlight`; if a lines-session response.done ever went missing on a healthy socket, app lines that wait on `voiceBusy` (bounded loops) would wait their full limits until the socket fails or reconnects.
