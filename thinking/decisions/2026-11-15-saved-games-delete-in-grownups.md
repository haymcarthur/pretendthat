# Deleting saved games lives in the Grown-ups corner, not on the shelf

**Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Who decided:** Haylee (a correction while the C/D design was being written)

- The kid-facing "My games" shelf has **no delete at all**: no long press to delete, no edit mode.
- Grown-ups delete saved games in the **Grown-ups corner**, behind a parental gate. It has a "Saved games" list where
  each delete needs a confirm.
- For now only the minimal corner is built (the gate + the Saved games manage/delete screen). It's structured so
  section E can add Manage subscription, Restore purchases, privacy and help later.
- Supersedes "long press + confirm on the shelf" in the paid-app checklist (section D) and the paid-app direction file.
- Design: [saved games + replay design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-saved-games-replay-design.md) §D6.
