## 1. iPhone chat + keyboard (fixed)
- The shelf's white background now ignores the bottom safe area and the keyboard (`PTChatPanelShape`, shelf look only), so
  the shelf stays flush with the screen's bottom edge and is white behind the keyboard (no gap under the keyboard's round
  corners). Its content still respects the keyboard, so only the input row (with the transcript above it) sits above the
  keyboard. The iPad's floating panel is unchanged.

## 2. In-game curtains reach the bottom (fixed, iPhone and iPad)
- Cause: the stage drew its curtain copy at the root's measured "screen" size, which on the phone is the safe-area height
  (778 pt on an 874 pt iPhone 18 Pro), so the curtains ended ~96 pt above the bottom and the floor showed under them. The
  iPad lost its 20 pt home-indicator strip the same way.
- Now the stage draws its curtains (and, on the phone, its sky and the "settle" tie-backs) down to the real bottom edge:
  the stage's own bottom plus its bottom inset (`curtainScreen` in `PTStageView`). Nothing else uses the new height, so
  every other layout (floor, Nutmeg's box, the top bar) is unchanged. On the phone the tie-backs now sit at 45% of the full
  screen, the same as the start screen (they were 45% of the shorter height), and the round-40 settle still raises them
  when she shrinks.

## 3. Step rail breathing room on the iPhone (built)
- The top bar sits 18 pt under the safe area (was 6 pt), and the compact rail has 8 pt above and below its steps (was 4),
  so the active step's ring no longer touches the rail's edge. The bar is 74 pt tall (was 68). Nutmeg's box starts that
  much lower (she is ~18 pt shorter on the phone at most). The X stays centered on the bar.

## 4. iPad: no zoom-out with the chat panel open (built)
- With the panel open, every card is pinned in the panel (Props, Print, the scene, The End), so the chat-open card preset
  (`propCardChat`) now returns the talking framing (`closeUp`, `fitClosed`), the exact framing she has while talking with
  the panel open. Opening Props, Print or a scene with the panel open no longer pulls the camera back (she was the medium
  shot scaled to ~0.68, small on a big platform; now ~1.6× bigger on the 13", same as talking).
- **Chat closed, not changed:** the prop card (and the kit papers, the scene card, The End) sits on the stage beside her,
  so she keeps the round-14 medium shot there. A close-up that leaves room for the card would make her head *smaller* than
  the medium shot (the lane beside the card is narrow), and a full close-up would put the card over her face. Her head in
  the chat-closed hunt is about the size it is when talking with the panel open. **For Haylee:** if "no zoom-out" was also
  meant for the chat-closed hunt, the option is the card drawn over her lower body (she stays in the talking close-up);
  say so and it's a small change.
- Tests: `chatOpenCardStepsKeepTheTalkingFraming` (same framing as talk, ≥1.3× the old scale, 11" and 13"); the kit test now
  expects the talk framing with the panel open; the other camera tests unchanged.

## 5. Role tiles for 4–6 players (fixed, iPad 11" and 13", iPhone)
- The Players step (the tap path: after the count, before "Let's go!") showed the roles in one row; with 4+ roles the row
  ran past the card zone (the 4th under the tie-back, the 5th and 6th off screen, the 1st over Nutmeg's face). Now up to 3
  roles stay in one row; 4 are 2 × 2 and 5–6 are three rows of two, so every tile is inside the card zone and tappable.
  The phone uses smaller tiles (112 pt, 88 pt for 5–6, 96 pt for 3 in a row) and its content zone is a little taller (380).
- The replay Pick (the site agent's report) has no role tiles: it shows the cover (and the 1–6 number tiles in two rows of
  three when there's no voice), which already fit. Checked on both iPads.

## 6. Writing hand: the vest shadow stays put (built: the simple fix, no new body)
- The ONE #470D62 path in nutmeg-writing-crayon.svg (the vest shadow) was moved, byte for byte, into a new asset
  `nutmeg-writing-crayon-shadow` (same 1178 × 1335 canvas, nothing else in it). `NutmegCharacter` draws it right under the
  crayon, in the writing body's transform, with no rock. The crayon and fingers still rock ±3.2° around the wrist.
- **Still frame:** rendered at 2× (macOS's SVG renderer, the same layer order and wrist pivot as the app): the 0° frame
  differs from before in 368 of 25.2 M channel values (~90 pixels), all on the anti-aliased seam where the shadow's edge
  meets the crayon's edge (it used to be drawn on top of the crayon, now under it); max difference 59/255 on those edge
  pixels, invisible at any normal size. Not strictly pixel-identical. The only exactly identical option (max 2/255) is the
  shadow drawn ABOVE the crayon, but then at +3.2° the shadow covers the crayon's lower edge as it rocks into it, which
  looks wrong, so it ships under the crayon as Haylee's note said.
- **Rock:** the shadow layer is the same at −3.2°, 0° and +3.2° (it never rocks); the crayon moves over it. At −3.2° the
  crayon lifts off a sliver of the shadow's own top edge (a small purple notch under the crayon's tip), which is the shadow
  on the vest showing, not a moving shadow.
- The new body (design/export/writing-pose/new-body-writing.svg) was not needed and is not used (fallback only).
