# D1: Planning is a hands-free voice conversation

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** Planning (pick a game → players → roles → props) is a hands-free voice conversation with Nutmeg, like ChatGPT voice mode. Nutmeg is big in the center of a calm, solid-color screen with a few floating theater/prop elements around her. No tapping and no hold-to-talk during planning. The only controls are **mute mic** and **leave activity**, in a ChatGPT-style bottom bar.

**Why:** Talking is how 4–8-year-olds already play pretend; tapping through screens felt like a form. A calm conversation screen also looks and feels more novel in the demo.
