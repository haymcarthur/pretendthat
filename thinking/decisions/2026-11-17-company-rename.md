# Rename the LLC to a neutral parent-company name before the App Store launch

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision (Haylee):** the publishing LLC (Utah, currently named after Tittl) gets a neutral, grown-up name that works over Tittl, PretendThat and future apps. The Apple Developer account is under the LLC; Tittl is also on Google Play.
- **Order (Claude's checklist, verify each office's current steps; not legal advice):**
  1. Pick a name and check it in the Utah LLC registry, USPTO trademarks and the domain (research/2026-11-17-company-name.md).
  2. File an LLC name amendment with the Utah Division of Corporations. A DBA isn't enough: the stores show the legal name.
  3. Tell the IRS about the name change (the EIN stays).
  4. Update the bank account name.
  5. Update the D-U-N-S record with Dun & Bradstreet (same number). Allow a week or more before Apple and Google see it.
  6. Apple: ask Developer Support to update the organization's legal entity name. Google Play: update the developer display name in Play Console, and the verified legal name / payments profile (may need re-verification).
  7. Update the operator name in the PretendThat privacy policy, terms and site footer, and fill in the policy's missing legal name and address.

## The fuller list of places to update (Claude, 2026-11-17; check each that applies)
- **Washington City business license:** yes. Update the licensed business name with the city (usually a change form; the business itself is the same).
- **Utah State Tax Commission:** if registered for any Utah tax account (sales tax, withholding), update the name in Taxpayer Access Point.
- **Registered agent:** if you use a paid agent, update their records.
- **Operating agreement:** amend or add a one-page resolution recording the new name.
- **IRS:** a name-change letter (the EIN stays). A new W-9 to Apple and Google with the new legal name.
- **Bank + business card:** the account name (needed for store payouts) and any business credit card.
- **D-U-N-S:** update the legal name (same number).
- **Apple:** the developer account legal entity name (Support); App Store Connect agreements, tax and banking show the new name.
- **Google Play:** the developer display name; the payments profile / verified organization name (may need re-verification); the tax info.
- **Trademarks:** if the LLC owns any (e.g. "Tittl"), record the owner's name change with the USPTO.
- **Vendors billed to the company:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Cloudflare, GitHub, Figma, TelemetryDeck, the domain registrar (WHOIS/contact), email, insurance, accounting software.
- **Public text:** the PretendThat privacy policy + terms + site footer, Tittl's privacy policy/terms/site, the app store listings' copyright line, and social profiles.

## The name: Crimson Foundry (Haylee, 2026-11-17)
- **New legal name:** **Crimson Foundry LLC**.
- **Why:** "Crimson" for southern Utah's red rock (Crimson Cliffs nearby). "Foundry" for a maker studio (like a type foundry), with a quiet nod to the McArthur family welding shop. It's neutral and grown-up, and it works over Tittl, PretendThat and future apps.
- **Screening (research/2026-11-17-company-name.md, Round 2):** no live US mark for CRIMSON FOUNDRY, no company or app-store seller with the name. crimsonfoundry.com is registered with no site (maybe for sale); .co/.app/.dev/.studio were free on 2026-11-17.
- **Caveats:**
  - Plain CRIMSON is crowded (240 live marks; Crimson Education owns CRIMSON in 9/41/42 and publishes apps).
  - Plain FOUNDRY is used by big software brands.
  - So: a trademark attorney's pre-filing search before relying on it.
- **Next steps (in order):**
  1. Haylee checks "Crimson Foundry" in the Utah registry (businessregistration.utah.gov/EntitySearch/OnlineEntitySearch).
  2. Optional attorney clearance.
  3. Optionally grab a domain (crimsonfoundry.co/.app/.studio, or ask about the .com).
  4. File the Utah LLC name amendment.
  5. Then everything else in the update list above (city license, Utah tax, operating agreement, IRS, W-9s, bank, D-U-N-S, Apple, Google Play, vendors, public text).
