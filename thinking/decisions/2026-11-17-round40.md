## 2. Setup and clean-up rows are goals (built; one live game)
- **What the kids see:** each row is a short goal in the game's world (2–6 words): "Get the clinic ready", "Close up
  the clinic". **What Nutmeg says** for the row (tap / speaker / "what do I do?"): the role, the goal, then the
  specifics: "Vet, get the clinic ready by hanging the Happy Paws Pet Vet sign by the door, stacking the Paw Bucks…".
- **Where the goals come from:** the guide call (the same one that writes the story and the setup / clean-up scenes)
  now writes, for each scene, one goal per ranked role R1–R6 (`setupScene.goals`, `cleanupScene.goals`). The item jobs
  no longer have short row texts (the rows show goals), and every job must start with a verb (prompts: guide, weave,
  re-ask). Missing or bad goals (not 2–6 words, quotes) are written by the app (`Bookends.repair`, no AI call).
- **How rows are made (app, per player count, no AI call):** items are grouped by the role their job suits; a playing
  kid's row = their goal + their items; another role's group becomes an extra row (that role's goal) while a kid has
  room (solo 3 rows, 2 players 2 each, 3+ players 1 each), else it joins the kid with the least to do; a kid with 3+
  more items than another hands one over; a row with more than 3 items splits while its kid has room (the new row
  takes a free role's goal, never a duplicate label); a kid with nothing takes one item. Spoken specifics turn each
  job into an -ing phrase ("hang" → "hanging", "set" → "setting", "count … and stack them up" → "counting … and
  stacking them up"); if a job doesn't start with a verb the app knows, the specifics follow as their own sentence
  ("…get the pet room ready! The sign goes up, then set up the pet bed").
- **Every prop AND every printed page is used on purpose, in code:** goal rows put every item (except the badges) in a
  setup row AND a clean-up row; the badges are in both opening lines ("Everyone, put on your badge first!" / "…take
  off your badge first!"). `SetupCoverage.setupMissing` / `cleanupMissing` check it; tests run every count 1–6, a
  2-player game's props, a swapped and a skipped prop.
- **Replays:** the goals are saved with the kit (`Bookend.goals`), and rows are a pure function of the saved kit, so a
  replay shows and says the same words (tested). **Older saved games** (no goals) still compose their round-37 rows
  exactly as before (no migration; `repair` never adds goals to them; the round-37 tests now run on the demo game with
  its goals taken off).
- **Demo game:** the bundled airplane game has goals now ("Get the cockpit ready", "Stock the snack cart", …).
- **Report a problem** shows the goals ("R1 goal: …") with the jobs.

### The live game (the round's one live game)
- 2 players, "pet vet", typed (`-PTLive`), PT-R15, **fake art** (`-PTFakeArt`: no image calls; this item is text
  only), to the setup scene, one row read aloud, then stopped.
- **Result:** the guide wrote all 12 goals itself (no repair, no re-ask). Setup: "Get the clinic ready" (Vet) and
  "Get your pet ready" (Pet Owner); clean-up: "Close up the clinic" / "Take your pet home". Every page and all 4
  props (table → pet exam table, towel → pet blanket, tray → check-in desk, stuffed animal → patient pet) were named
  in both scenes; the coverage log is in `live/console.log` (`COVERAGE`).
- **Found two problems, fixed after it (unit-tested on the same shape, not re-run live):** (1) the Vet's one row had 5
  items, a 16-second line ("heard 16.2 s"): rows now split at more than 3 items when the kid has room, and kids get
  fair shares, so this game would now show 4 rows of 2–3 items; (2) "sit the patient pet…" isn't a verb the app knew,
  so that row fell back to the "Goal! Do this, then that" form, and "count … and stack them up" didn't turn "stack"
  into "stacking": more verbs, and a verb after "and" followed by "them / the / it / up…" becomes -ing too.
- **Cost:** Claude 4 calls, 19,606 in / 4,631 out ≈ **17.1¢** (pages 3.26¢, guide 7.64¢, two weaves 3.01¢ + 3.19¢;

  Images 0, moderation free. **Total ≈ 53¢.** Secrets.xcconfig was copied in for this build only and deleted right

## 3. Replay Pick shows the whole cover (fixed, iPhone + iPad)
- Cause: the phone's cover zone (270 pt) was shorter than the cover card (360 × 240 picture + mat), so the card was
  clipped at the bottom; the picture was also `scaledToFill` at a fixed 3:2.
- Now the card takes the picture's own shape (clamped 1:1 to 2:1) with the whole picture fit inside, and its width
  shrinks to fit the card zone's height (with the number tiles under it when they show); the phone's zone is a bit
  taller (300 pt; 500 with tiles). iPad checked: unchanged size, whole picture.

## 4. iPhone stage when Nutmeg shrinks (built: "settle")
- **Compared** (sheet-stage-compare.png): round 39 (the floor shrinks with her: a little round platform mid-screen, a
  straight sky line beside it, tie-backs half behind the card) / **camera zoom-out** (the whole proscenium, sky, floor
  and curtains shrink with her inside the coral curtains) / **settle**.
- **Shipped: settle.** She still shrinks and steps back, but the floor stays as wide as the stage (its edge is a
  gentle curve right under her feet, so there's no small platform and no straight line), and the curtains' tie-backs
  rise with her (they keep their height above the floor in her scale), so they sit beside her, above the card. The
  card then covers the lower stage cleanly. The stage draws its own sky and valance on the phone so its curtains
  (with the moved tie) are the only curtains.
- **Why not the zoom-out:** it reads as a small stage inside a big coral frame: thin curtain strips and tiny tassels,
  her platform a mound again, a lot of coral, and the valance no longer matches the shrunken curtains. Settle keeps
  the scene full-screen and calm at every size.
- iPhone only (the iPad never takes this path). The comparison looks stay behind `-PTStageLook zoom|old` (DEBUG).
