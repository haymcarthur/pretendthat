## 1. Replay prop lines (Haylee)
- **The app now writes every prop line, on first plays AND replays** (`PropLines`, NutmegCore): "First, can you find a
  pot? It'll be the captain's hat! <reason> Tap the green check when you find it, or the X if you don't have one!", then
  rotating phrasings with no "Next," ("Can you find …? It'll be …!", "Ooh, a … would make a great …! Can you find one?",
  "Now let's look for …", "How about …? It can be …!", and every 3rd prop "Let's pretend that it's …" (round 13)). The
  lead says the answer before it: a cheer after a found prop (rotating by number, deterministic), "No problem, we'll
  pretend the …!" after a missing one. The words depend only on the prop, its number and the answer before it, so a
  replay with the same answers says the same words and **plays the first play's recorded clip** (clip key = normalized
  words, D3b). Never "do you still have".
- **Claude's call (how):** on a live first play the model still PICKS each prop (show_next_prop), but its tool output
  now says "Say NOTHING: the app says the prop line itself: \"…\"" (respond = false); the app says it through the
  speaker once her turn is quiet (`sayAppPropLine`), recorded per D3b (it's an app line, so D3b's "only app lines are
  recorded" rule holds). The first prop line follows the roles line the same way (the session no longer asks her for it;
  a silent note tells her the words). Tap and voice paths both. The swap line ("a towel works too") stays hers on a first
  play. Without a live session (tests, tap fallback) the model says the lines as before, and its examples no longer
  start with "Next".
- **Replay:** the count line and the first prop line are now two lines (the first prop's clip plays again).
- **Measured (PT-R15, "Pet Shop with Puppies and Kittens", 2 kids):** first play recorded 4 prop lines (heard 10.8 /
  5.9 / 8.7 / 6.6 s). The replay with the same answers played prop lines 2 and 4 from those clips; line 3 differed
  (the first play swapped that card; the replay's saved item is the swap) and was said live once and saved; line 1 was
  glued to the count line (fixed after; run C: the first prop line played from its first-play clip, 3/4 clips). Replay totals: clips 7/11, 5 Realtime
  responses ≈ $0.077, 0 content AI calls.

## 3. In-app speech model download (Haylee)
- `SpeechModelInstaller` (app): iPadOS 26+ only (`if #available(iOS 26, *)`; deployment target stays 17.0): checks
  `AssetInventory.status(forModules: [DictationTranscriber(en-US, .shortDictation)])`, downloads with
  `assetInstallationRequest(supporting:)` + `downloadAndInstall()`, progress from the request's `Progress`. States and
  copy are in NutmegCore (`SpeechModelState`, `SpeechModelCopy`: "Nutmeg needs to learn to listen, one time … About a
  minute on Wi-Fi").
- Started **quietly at onboarding's mic step** once both permissions are granted (if the iPad can't already listen).
- A mic press while the model is missing opens the one-tap grown-up sheet (`SpeechModelSheet`: Download now → progress
  → All set; "Type instead for now"). Older iPadOS (and an unsupported language) keep the Settings › Dictation note.
- **Claude's call:** the downloaded model is SpeechAnalyzer's, which the old `SFSpeechRecognizer` may not use, so a kid
  turn goes through a new `AnalyzerTurn` (SpeechAnalyzer + DictationTranscriber, same pre-roll, same buffers, bounded
  finish) ONLY when the model is installed and `SFSpeechRecognizer` can't recognize on the device. Where round 31's path
  already works, nothing changes. On-device only; the audio is dropped after the turn.
- **Simulator:** the models can't load here; `-PTSpeechModel needs|downloading|failed|installed|old` (+
  `-PTSpeechModelSheet`, `-PTSpeechModelFake`) show the UI states. **Needs the real iPad:** the real download, its
  progress and time, and that AnalyzerTurn hears kids.

## 4. Scene tasks: short on screen, specifics spoken (Haylee)
- Schema: each role task (R1–R6, solo) is `{short, say}` (`KitScenesSix`); the array schema gets `short` too.
  `Step.short` is new (optional, older saved games derive one), `Step.text` is the spoken `say`. Prompt: short = 3–7
  words, broad, no role title; say = the title, the job, 1–2 concrete ideas with their props/kit, ~15–30 words.
- `GuideRepair` step 6 (`TaskShort`): every short is 2–7 words, no role title, no quotes or end punctuation; a missing
  or long one is cut down from the spoken text ("Pet Trainer, it's time to get the pet ready to go home! …" → "Get the
  pet ready to go home"). Real props, no scripted lines, skips/swaps/"pretend" now apply to BOTH texts. Weave max 40 words.
- Scene opening: ONLY the setup + "Tap your speaker when you're ready to hear your job!" (+ the first scene's ▶ how-to).
  Repeat says the same. Voice "what do I do, Pet Trainer?" → new tool `read_job` (scene step only) reads that row's say.
- **Measured (heard seconds, her real voice, 2 kids):** new openings 8.9 s (scene 1, with the ▶ how-to) / 4.7 / 5.2 s.
  Before (`-PTOldSceneOpen`, the round 29 read-out, same game at 6 kids): 23.7 / 20.2 / 21.8 s heard. Voice cost per
  opening: 578 / 490 / 531 audio-out tokens before → 223 / 123 / 136 after (about −60 to −75%); each row's say is then
  only spoken when a kid taps it (5–7 s, recorded once like every app line). Guide output grows (live: 1,035 → ~1,700 tokens, 3.7¢ → 5.7¢).
- Seen once in run A: the early guide came back with empty titles/setups (372 output tokens, twice); GuideRepair
  filled them ("Let the Fun Begin"). Not reproduced in 4 direct calls + 1 more app run (`-PTLogGuide` logs the answer).
