# No bookshelf moment; the End line says the game is saved

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** remove the round 39 bookshelf moment (the card flying to the shelf and "I put it on your bookshelf!"). The game still auto-saves silently when the kit is made. At The End Nutmeg says: "The end! What a show! Take a bow! This game is saved, so you can play it again later. Tap the arrow to play it again, or the house to go home!"
- **Why:** kids don't know what "the bookshelf" means, especially on their first game. "Saved so you can play it again" is clear. This supersedes the line wording approved earlier the same day.
- **Who decided:** Haylee.
