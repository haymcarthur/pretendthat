# D5: Generated sticker illustrations, no icons or emoji

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** No icons or emoji for props and items on screen. Each prop, role or game idea gets a generated flat, sticker-style illustration in the brand style (OpenAI image model; flat vector sticker, bold simple shapes, brand palette, white outline, transparent background, no text). A soft shimmer shows until each one is ready. Supersedes C9 (emoji pictures) for the conversation screen.

**Why:** Emoji look generic; matching stickers look like one designed product (aesthetics is 20% of judging).
