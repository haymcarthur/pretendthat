# Nutmeg sounds like the high greeting clip, not her live voice

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** The bright recorded greeting clip (~447 Hz heard) is what Nutmeg should sound like. Her live Realtime lines (~260–340 Hz heard) are too low, so the live voice gets raised to match, not the clips lowered.
- **Why:** Haylee's recording (thinking/research/2026-11-17-voice-drift.md) showed the "gets lower" feeling is the switch from the high clip to the live voice, about 9.5 semitones lower in the same breath. The speed is a true 1.20 throughout. Varispeed alone can't close a gap that big without making her far too fast, so it needs a pitch raise apart from speed.
- **Next:** an offline listening test first (pitch-only raise at several amounts on top of 1.20). Nothing ships until Haylee picks by ear. Also add a pitch check on every recorded clip.
- **Who decided:** Haylee (direction); the listening test first is Claude's call.
