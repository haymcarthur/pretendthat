# No grown-up gate in onboarding (2026-11-16)

**Decision:** First-launch onboarding has no grown-up check. It opens straight on "Hi, grown-up!" and continues from there.

**Why:** It's the grown-up who downloads the app and opens it the first time, so a gate at the start just adds friction. The app isn't in the Kids Category, so Apple doesn't require a gate there. The hold-then-tap-the-number gate stays where a kid could plausibly tap: the Grown-ups menu, and any purchase or subscription change made after onboarding (Plans in the Grown-ups menu, the paywall when the free shows run out).

**Who decided:** Haylee (no gate at the start); Claude's call to keep the gate on the in-app Grown-ups menu and later purchases.
