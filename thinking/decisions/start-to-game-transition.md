# Start screen → game transition, and one shared stage

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee (behavior); implementation details Claude's call

1. On the start screen Nutmeg stays IN the chest. Tapping "Let's play!" runs a ~1.25 s exit (`PTStartExit`): card and
   button pop away (scale down + fade), Nutmeg crouches and hops up and out of the chest (idle pose with her
   clipboard, tail now shown), growing on the way, and lands on the floor exactly where the conversation screen draws
   her (medium shot with the rail and pill showing). The chest drops away off the bottom and the toys scoot off the
   sides as she lands. A tap during the exit does nothing extra. Reduce Motion: straight to the game (crossfade).
2. The game screens use the same stage: `PTStageBackdrop` (sky, wooden floor oval with a flat darker rim, tied-back
   curtains, valance) replaces the plain off-white canvas under the conversation, print and play screens. UI chrome
   (rail, pill, cards, panels) stays white and crisp on top.
3. Hand-off (Claude's call): rather than sharing one view instance across screens (matchedGeometryEffect across
   different transforms and hierarchies was fragile), the start screen computes the conversation layout with the same
   `PTStageCamera.layout` and lands her on that exact frame; RootView then swaps the start screen for the
   conversation with NO fade (identical frame and pose, so the swap is invisible). The rail and pill slide in from
   their edges as before. If voice isn't available (tap-screen fallback) the classic layout takes over; Nutmeg moves
   to her column there.
4. No pose swaps on the start screen (Haylee): the idle pose (with clipboard) for the whole intro and exit; the wave
   was dropped from the intro. `nutmeg-body-idle-notail` / `-wave-notail` (generated by tools/import_art.py) hide her
   tail while she stands in the chest.
