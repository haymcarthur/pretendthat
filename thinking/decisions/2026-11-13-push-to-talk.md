# Push-to-talk instead of an always-on mic

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** Kids press and hold a big red microphone button to talk; letting go sends it and Nutmeg answers. No always-on listening. Control pill becomes: chat (left) · big red mic (center) · close (right). Tapping close shows a warning that leaving means the game can't be resumed where they left off.

**Why:** An always-on mic fills the transcript with background chatter and makes Nutmeg answer when nobody was talking to her. Hold-to-talk solves both and makes the addressee detection unnecessary.

**Update (Haylee, later 2026-11-13):** sides swapped: **close on the LEFT, chat on the RIGHT**, big red mic in the center. The white backing hugs the buttons: a circle around the mic narrowing to a shorter pill behind the side buttons, with smooth concave transitions and tighter spacing (~10 pt).
