# Positioning: game master for real-world pretend play

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Claude's call, Haylee agreed

**Decision:** Not an AI friend (WimziPal) and not a coloring app (Boxley). The app gathers the siblings, assigns roles, turns household items into props, makes a matching personalized kit, guides play, then steps back.

**Why:** White space from the [competitor analysis](../research/competitor-analysis.md): no generated per-game kits, no role assignment for several kids, no prop-substitution app, and no product that hands play off the screen.
