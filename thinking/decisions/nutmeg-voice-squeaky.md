# D7: Nutmeg's voice: ChatGPT-like, higher and squeakier

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** Nutmeg sounds like ChatGPT's voices but higher-pitched and squeakier. Implementation (Claude's call): a playful Realtime voice steered by the session instructions, plus an on-device pitch raise with `AVAudioUnitTimePitch` (default +350 cents). Voice and pitch are adjustable in Grown-ups so Haylee can tune them.

**Why:** She's a little mouse; a squeak sells the character.
