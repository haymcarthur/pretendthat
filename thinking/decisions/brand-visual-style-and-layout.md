# Brand: visual style, palette and layout

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:**
- **Style:** bold, flat vector, friendly, with big expressive eyes. Main references: the "Как белка" (Like a Squirrel) app and Duolingo; Easy Chinese for Kids for energy. Not 3D clay, not chaotic teen doodles.
- **Palette (approved):** coral-red (curtains), deep purple (backstage), sunny yellow (spotlight), lime (success/checked).
- **Layout:** no detailed full-scene backgrounds. Screens use solid backgrounds (some white, some solid brand colors) with lots of white space. Theater props (curtain corner, spotlight, ticket, star) sit around the mascot as accents, like Как белка and Duolingo. A screen can be a big close-up of the mascot's face on a solid color. Subtle ambient motion stays on the props (curtain sway, spotlight pulse).

**Supersedes:** the palette and the "AI-generated backgrounds and scene art" part of [Brand: backstage toy theater](brand-backstage-theater.md). The theater world and the motion idea stay.

**Why:** Haylee's taste, matched to the reference apps she likes most. Clean screens keep the mascot the focus.
