## 8. Props are never things the kit prints (built, prompt + code)
- **Code** (`PrintedProps`, NutmegCore): a prop's pretend use is checked two ways:
  1. By word: the head noun of "becomes" (the last word before "of/for/with…", plurals folded) is a printed kind of thing:
     tickets, passes/boarding, keycards, cards, coupons, passports, receipts, money/cash/dollars/bills/coins/bucks/tokens,
     menus/price lists, signs/posters/banners/labels, badges/tags/name tags/stickers, maps, letters, notes, scrolls,
     invitations, stamps, pages, paper, charts. "the ticket booth" or "the money box" are fine (the head noun is a booth, a box).
  2. By THIS kit: once the kit pages exist (they're written during the hunt), the use may not be one of this kit's own
     printed things: the tickets/money/menu titles' nouns ("Cloud Bucks") and every cut-out ("Suitcase", "Juice Boxes").
     The sign's title (the place name) and badge roles don't count.
- `PropFit.fits` rejects printed uses (so Claude's tap-path prop list is repaired too), `show_next_prop` and `swap_prop`
  give the model ONE retry with the reason ("…is a paper thing the printed kit makes…"), then the app swaps in a sensible
  non-printed pair (the alternates and the item first). The curated pairs that were printed things were replaced
  (paper: the fan / the snowballs; sticky notes: the leaves / the spots; towel: the magic carpet; books: the castle wall;
  sheet: the tent; no gold coins on plates). DEBUG builds log every turned-away prop ("PROP rejected …").
- **Prompts:** the realtime instructions have "PROPS ARE NEVER PRINTED THINGS" (never "paper as the boarding passes"),
  `show_next_prop` says it, and Claude's props prompt says it. The old "a map is flat (towel, sheet, books)" examples and
  "pretend that it's the treasure map!" are gone (maps are printed).
- Tests: `Round42Tests` (24 uses by word, "paper → boarding passes" rejected by `fits`, by `show_next_prop` (retry, then a
  non-printed fallback) and by `swap_prop`; kit nouns (a "Suitcase" cut-out blocks "the suitcase" only in that kit); no
