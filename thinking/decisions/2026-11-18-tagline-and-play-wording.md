# Tagline "Plan it, print it, play it" and accurate Play wording

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decision:** Replace "Pretend play, off the screen" with **"Plan it, print it, play it"** everywhere (App Store subtitle, site hero, slide 1, header creative, app onboarding). The Play step is "**Play it out, scene by scene**" / "Nutmeg sets each scene. Kids act it out." One flow sentence everywhere: "Kids plan a game by talking with Nutmeg, hunt for props around the house, print a kit made for their game, and act it out together, scene by scene."
- **Why:** Haylee: "off the screen" is misleading (kids still get instructions on screen between scenes); the old flow sentence mixed verb forms. Tagline: Haylee liked all four options and asked for the best performer; Claude's pick (says what the app does; "print" is a differentiator and a search term; short and rhythmic; matches the search image).
