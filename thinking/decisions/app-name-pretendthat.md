# App name: PretendThat

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** The app is called **PretendThat**.

**Why:** It's what Haylee's kids actually say when they start a game: "pretend that I'm the pilot." It names the behavior the app supports (real-world make-believe), not a screen activity.

**Alternatives considered:**
- **Boxley:** taken (boxley.app, a cardboard-robot printables app). See [name check](../research/name-check-boxley.md).
- **Snipsqueak:** rejected; it plays on "pipsqueak", which Haylee finds derogatory.
- **PropCue:** runner-up.
- **PlayKit:** taken (Seenaryo Playkit; Lovevery Play Kits).

**Availability (quick check):** the `.app` domain looked free; the `.com` is registered but parked. No trademark search done.
