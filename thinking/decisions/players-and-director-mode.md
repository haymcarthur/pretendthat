# Up to 4 players, Director mode for 5+

> **Superseded** by [Roles, not names](roles-not-names.md) (Haylee, 2026-11-13): only the number of kids is asked (no names); up to 8 kids, 5+ share 4 roles (e.g. 2 Passengers). Director mode is removed.

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** 1–4 named players, each with a role. With 5 or more kids, one "Director" holds the tablet and runs every checklist, and badges print with a blank name line. 1 player is allowed (stuffed animals fill the other roles).

**Why:** Keeps hand-off manageable.
