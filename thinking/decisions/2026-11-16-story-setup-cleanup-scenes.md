# Story opener, setup scene and clean-up scene (2026-11-16)

**Decision:** After printing, Nutmeg tells a short story opener (~15 s). Scene 1 is always setup: one task per role that sets up the stage using the props and the printed pieces, with every prop and printed item used for a clear purpose (nothing random). Then 3 play scenes as today, then a clean-up scene framed in the story (one task per role, real props put away), then The End.

**Why:** Gives kids a reason to play (the story), turns props and printouts into a stage they built (setup), and ends the night in-story so the grown-up doesn't have to (clean-up, also a selling point). Cost about +$0.05–0.10 per new game; replays reuse recorded clips.

**Who decided:** Haylee (the idea, 3 play scenes, every item must have a purpose); Claude's calls: ~15 s opener, one quick task per role for setup/clean-up, code validation that every item is used.
