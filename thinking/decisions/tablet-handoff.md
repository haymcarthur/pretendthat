# Tablet hand-off with color and role badges

> **Updated** by [Roles, not names](roles-not-names.md) (Haylee, 2026-11-13): hand-offs are by role, "Pass the tablet to the Captain!" in the role color. No names.

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Claude's call, Haylee agreed

**Decision:** Each kid gets a color and role badge (also printed on their items). The game is split into scenes owned by roles. Nutmeg says "Pass the tablet to Captain Mia!", and the next kid taps their badge to take over.

**Why:** Non-readers recognize color plus icon. Turns follow the story, not a timer.
