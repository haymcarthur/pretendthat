# Plans: Show Pass 5 games, Season Pass 20 games, no intro offers

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision (locked):**
  - **Free Preview:** 2 new games, ONCE (lifetime, never per month). Must read clearly as "2 free games to try", never "2 a month", everywhere (app, onboarding, paywall, site, App Store text).
  - **Show Pass:** 5 new games a month. $9.99/month or $99.99/year.
  - **Season Pass:** 20 new games a month. $24.99/month or $249.99/year.
  - **No introductory offers** (remove the 4 already set up in App Store Connect and in the app's StoreKit config/copy).
  - Saved games always free to replay. Yearly = 16.6% off on both ("12 months for the price of 10").
- **Why:** a measured live game cost ~$0.83 (≈$0.92 with color-in printables). At 10/30 games the Season Pass (and its intros) lost money for families who use most of their games. 5/20 keeps a real ladder (Show $2/game, Season $1.25/game monthly) and every monthly plan profitable at full use (Show +$3.89, Season +$2.84 a month after Apple's 15%). Season yearly loses ~69¢/mo only if all 20 games are used every month; accepted for matching discounts.
- **Requires:** enrolling in Apple's App Store Small Business Program (15% from day one; at 30% the Season monthly is ~−$0.91 at full use).
- **Replaces:** the 10/30 sizes and the intro prices (decisions 2026-11-17-yearly-intro-sonnet.md, paid-app-direction pricing).
- **Who decided:** Haylee (5/20, prices, no intros, "2 forever" clarity); Claude (the math).
