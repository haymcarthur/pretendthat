# Scenes become "checklists, then a short scenario" (2026-11-20)

**Why:** in a real family game (build 4) the ensemble tasks were too long. The kids replayed them over and over to remember everything. Short checklist items fit how 4–8 year olds listen: hear one thing, do it, come back for the next.

**Decision (Haylee):**
- Each scene opens with **one checklist per playing role, all on screen at once**, so kids work in parallel (one listens while another does a job).
- **Checklist card per role:** the role picture and role name at the top. Each item has a **checkbox on the left** and a **🔊 speaker on the right**. Checkboxes come back (the ensemble scenes had removed them).
- Items are simple setup jobs for that scene. Example, "Get the rocket ready": pilot puts on the badge, sets up the control panel, sets out the seats.
- When every checklist is done, Nutmeg gives the **scenario. Keep it very short:** just enough of a "Pretend that…" prompt to start their imagination. They act it out, then move to the next scene.
- **The scene explanation at the start of each scene is removed** (the scenario now comes at the end of setup).
- **More players, fewer scenes.**

**Claude's proposals (Haylee answered the scenario length, checkboxes and card layout; the rest go ahead unless she objects):**
- A one-line cue when a scene opens ("Scene 2: Get the rocket ready! Tap your picture to hear your jobs").
- The scenario is a few seconds long (one "Pretend that…" line plus a hint of what might happen), with a 🔊 to hear it again.
- **Update (Haylee, 2026-11-20):** the scenario never ends on just a problem. It ends with an action for everyone, e.g. "…uh-oh, the control panel is beeping! Everyone, do your job to get the rocket safely into space!"
- Taps while she's talking queue up, and the row being read is highlighted.
- Items can be actions too ("put on your helmet"), but no busywork.
- Scenes by count: 1–2 kids → 4, 3–4 → 3, 5–6 → 2. Generate 4 in one call and drop scenes for bigger groups, so a count change still needs no AI call. 2–3 items per role per scene.
- The setup scene is folded into the per-scene checklists, and props and printouts are spread across scenes (every item is still used). The clean-up scene stays as a final checklist with no scenario.
- Where Nutmeg goes with many checklists: still open (see open-questions.md, 2026-11-20).

**Layout (Haylee, 2026-11-20, after the first mockups in design/mockups/checklist-scenes/):**
- **iPad:** Nutmeg sits on one of the checklist cards (peeking over a card), not on the stage.
- **iPhone:** setup opens a shelf like the chat shelf, holding all the checklists, and Nutmeg peeks over the shelf.
- **◀ Previous / ▶ Next scene:** centered on the screen as a pair, both the same size (the first mockup's mismatched pair looked broken).
