## 1. The guide (NutmegCore: `OneScene.swift`)
- **One guide call** (`PhaseService.earlyGuide` → `OneScene.schema()` + `OneScene.prompt`, decoded by `OneSceneGuide`): the story, a setup title, a **setup checklist** (2–3 short items for each of R1–R6, "Put on your badge" first, the pages spread over the roles), the scene's **"Pretend that…" opener**, an **event pool of 8–12 events**, a **finale** for everyone, a **music family** and the clean-up title.
- **An event** = `mood` (calm | tense) + the `roles` it calls (1–3) + one short `line` naming the role(s) and one thing to do + (tense only) a `calmAgain` line that says it's over **without crediting the kids**.
- **Stored as ONE guide scene** (Claude's call): its steps are the setup items (so the prop weave, `realProps`, the kid-safety check and saved games keep working on it) and `Scene.beats` holds the opener, the pool, the finale and the music. `Kit.isOneScene` tells it apart; older kits never have `beats`.
- **Music families (fixed list):** `space-adventure` (Space adventure), `jungle-outdoors` (Jungle adventure), `magical` (Magic kingdom), `cozy-shop` (Cozy shop, the default), `spooky-silly` (Silly spooky), `under-the-sea` (Under the sea), `desert-western` (Wild west). Parsed tolerantly; an unknown tag falls back to a keyword guess from the game's words, then Cozy shop.
- **Repair** (`OneScene.repair`, through `GuideRepair.repair`; idempotent, no AI call): a real setup title; 1–3 clean items per role (a role with none gets two from its job); only real objects; the opener says "Pretend that"; the finale speaks to everyone; **an event's roles are the roles its line names** (a longer title first, so "VIP Passenger" isn't also "Passenger"); a line naming no role gets its listed roles' titles in front (or "Everyone,"); more than 3 named roles drops it; a tense event's calm-again that credits the kids ("you fixed it", "great job") or is missing becomes "Phew, everything is calm again." (or one of two others); duplicates dropped; at most 12 kept.
- **The prompt makes the pool work for every count** (Claude's call): at least 3 events call only R1 (one tense), at least 2 call R2, every role R3–R6 is called by at least 2 events that may also call higher-ranked roles, and a few call 2–3 roles.
- **Picking for N kids** (`OneScene.pick`, every count 1–6 tested): 3 events for 1–2 kids, 4 for 3–4, 5 for 5–6; the cadence calm → tense → calm → tense → calm (each tense one followed by its calm-again). Only events whose roles are all playing are used. **Fair calls are counted in code:** every playing role is called at least once and the most-called role has at most one call more than the least (an "everyone" event counts for all). The best fair set wins (fewest app-written events, closest to the cadence, earliest in the pool). If the pool can't be fair, app-written events for the least-called roles are swapped in one at a time ("Captain and Pilot, uh-oh, something is beeping! Quick, go check it out!" / "…, take a look around and make sure everything is ready!"); if that still fails, an all app-written round-robin (always fair). The bundled game's pool is fair at every count with no app event (tested).
- **Composed arc** (`OneScene.compose`, through `PlayCast.kit`): story → **setup checklist** (`kind .setup`, the playing roles' cards; every printed page and every prop the kids have is in a setup item they see, app-added when missing, as in the 11-20 round; `OneSceneCoverage`) → **the scene** (`Scene.isBeatScene`: the picked events in order) → **clean-up checklist**.
- **Saved games: schema 7.** Schemas 2–6 still load and replay their old paths (tested: a schema 6 checklist game loads into the checklist path). `ResumePoint` gains `sceneTime` (scene seconds; game content only; the privacy test lists it).
- **Bundled game:** `airplane-onescene.json` (`Fallback.oneScenes`, `oneSceneAirplane`): the airplane setup checklist + 12 events + finale, music `space-adventure` (Claude's call: the closest family to a flight; none is "sky"). The store's fallback and demo mode use it (`AdventureStore.useOneSceneFallback`, default on).

## 2. Play flow (app)
- **Setup checklist:** the 11-20 cards (check left, 🔊 right, the reading queue, Nutmeg peeking on the iPad / her head on the iPhone), titled with the setup title (no "Scene 1:"). Its cue: "Get the Plane Ready! Tap your picture to hear your jobs." The only button besides the 🔊/checks is **▶ Let's play!** (`PTOneSceneButton`): grey and inert until every playing card is done, then green with a halo. No ◀ / ▶ pair in a one-scene game.
- **The scene:** the step rail hides; only the small ✕ (left) and the labeled **⏸ Pause** pill (right; in the top bar on the iPhone); the **music pill** at the top ("Space adventure", "· uh-oh!" in coral while it's tense); a **thin scene bar** at the bottom with a dot per event (filled once it has happened) and a star for the finale. Nutmeg stands centered, whole body (the stage's own `.hop` framing) while they play, and moves to the prop-card framing (her medium shot, the card zone on her right; under her on the iPhone) while a card is up. No other buttons.
- **Timeline** (`BeatTimeline` / `BeatRun` / `BeatClock`, NutmegCore, tested with an injected clock): 10 minutes; the opener at 0; the first event at 1:30; events evenly spaced up to the finale (3 events ≈ 2:35 apart, 4 ≈ 1:56, 5 ≈ 1:33); each event line repeated once by herself 15 s later; a tense event's calm-again 45 s after it; the finale at 9:15; the clean-up opens 20 s after the finale. A cue fires once; after a hold-up a stale repeat is dropped when the next event is also due. (Claude's numbers; change after a family test.)
- **An event:** the chime (the music ducks), then Nutmeg says the line (0.9 s later), and the big display-only card shows for 30 s: role picture(s) + name(s) ("Pilot + Engineer") + the line; a tense event has the warmer style (coral border, yellow "!" badge, a gently pulsing halo; still with reduced motion). **Calm-again:** a soft green card with the line for 12 s; the calm music returns. **Finale:** the celebrate gesture, confetti, the win music, her line; then the clean-up checklist opens by itself.
- **Clean-up:** the same cards; **▶ The End** lights up when everything is checked (no automatic The End in a one-scene game) → The End.
- **Pause:** stops the music, the scene clock and her speech (`UserAction.pauseScene`); the stage dims under the **"Paused" card** ("Music stopped. Nutmeg waits for you.") with a big ▶ Resume. A line cut off by the pause isn't said again (Claude's call). **Going to the background pauses the scene too** (Claude's call: the kids come back to the Paused card, not a scene that ran on without them).
- **Resume after a relaunch:** the resume point now keeps the scene time (saved every 5 s and on pause); the fired events are every cue before it (`BeatRun.restore`, so nothing is said again). The setup and clean-up keep their checks as before. **A resumed scene opens paused** (Claude's call), so the family presses ▶ Resume when they're back in place.
- **The microphone stays off for the whole Play phase** (setup, scene, clean-up) and The End: `PlayMic.allowed` (NutmegCore, tested) drives the hidden control bar (`RootView.playNoBar`) and `AppModel.talkPressed` / `micPressed` refuse a press during Play. The music player never touches the input node.

## 3. Music engine
- `PretendThat/Voice/MusicPlayer.swift`: per family a calm loop, a tense loop and a win ending, plus one shared chime. **Same approach as the writing scribble (`ScribbleSound`)**: plain `AVAudioPlayer`s on the app's current audio session; it never configures or activates the session, never touches NutmegSpeaker's engine or `SpeakerGraph`, never the mic. So it can't fight her voice or the voice-stall fixes (Claude's call over a second node on her engine, which is rebuilt on route changes and interruptions).
- Loops use `numberOfLoops = -1`; calm ↔ tense crossfade 1.5 s at the event boundaries; the win ending plays once from its start; **ducking −12 dB** (`MusicMix`, tested) under her speech and the chime, on a 0.25 s ramp, checked 4× a second.
- **Bundle layout:** `PretendThat/Resources/Music/<family>/calm.m4a | tense.m4a | win.m4a` and `Music/chime.m4a`, a **folder reference** in project.yml (the seven families share file names). Documented in `Resources/Music/README.md`. **Missing files = silence**; the clock and events run either way.
- **Placeholders:** `tools/make_placeholder_music.py` (numpy + ffmpeg) wrote soft sine-tone loops in each family's own key, ~6–14 KB each (≈ 250 KB total), tagged PLACEHOLDER in the files and the README. Replace with the real music under the same names.
- Known limit: `AVAudioPlayer` loops AAC well but not always sample-exact; if a seam clicks with the real music, move the loops to an `AVAudioPlayerNode` with `scheduleBuffer(.loops)` (the research doc's recipe) on its own engine.

## 4. Clean-up checklist
- `OneScene.cleanupScene`: **one item for every prop the kids got out** (found or swapped; a skipped prop is a printed cut-out) + **exactly one print-mess item** ("Pick up paper scraps, crayons and scissors"). Each item goes to the least busy playing role (ties: the role it suits, then rank), so **no role has more than one item more than another** (tested at every count 1–6 with all props, skipped props, extra props and none). A role with nothing (fewer items than kids) gets "Give high fives". Prop jobs come from the weave's clean-up job, else the app's.

## 5. Expressions
- New `NutmegState`s: `grooving` (calm play: happy, a small head nod on each beat at ~96 BPM and a slow sway, tail swinging along; nothing with reduced motion; built into the existing `Motion` rig like the writing nod), `calling` (calm event: talking + perked ears), `alarmed` (tense event), `relieved` (calm-again); the finale uses `excited` + the celebrate gesture; paused = plain happy.
- New `NutmegFace` cases `alarmed` and `phew` with `alarmedAvailable` / `phewAvailable` (the `focusAvailable` pattern): without `nutmeg-face-alarmed` / `nutmeg-face-phew`, alarmed = ooh + perked ears and phew = happy. Their mouths use `-mouth` assets when present, else ooh / happy. **The asset catalog already has both faces and their mouths** (art commits 6e19bc0, 973e9b3 and later, made in parallel with this round), so they show now; the fallback only matters if they're removed.

## Review pass (2026-11-20 evening, Claude's call; no build, no live call, $0)
Every new or changed app file was read against the definitions it calls (NutmegCore `OneScene`, `StageCamera`, `SpeechStop`, `AdventureStore`, Theme, `PTNextSceneButton.Style`, `PTCharacterPicture`, `ClipStore.has`). Every switch over an enum that gained cases (`NutmegState`, `NutmegFace`, `UserAction`, `BeatDisplay`) is exhaustive. All edited files pass `swiftc -parse`. NutmegCore wasn't touched (no harness run needed).

**Fixed:**
- **Auto-lock in the middle of the scene** (the real bug): auto-lock was allowed in Play, so after a few untouched minutes the iPad locked, the app went to the background and the scene paused itself. `AppModel.updateIdleTimer` now keeps the screen awake while the one scene runs (not while it's paused); the scene's start, end, pause and resume update it.
- **Music after an interruption:** a calm/tense loop the system stopped (Siri, an alarm, the voice touching the session) stayed silent until the next segment change. `MusicPlayer.play` restarts the current loop on the next tick (loops only).
- **Resume during the finale:** ▶ Resume replayed a win ending that had already finished; now only what was playing at the pause goes on.
- **Chime duck:** the music stayed down only 1.2 s after the chime, so it swelled back before a live line started (0.9 s + network). Now 2.5 s; her speech keeps it down after that.
- **Phew face blink:** the blink swapped her closed phew eyes for the nap eyes (yay was already excluded). The alarmed/phew mouth checks are looked up once (`alarmedMouthAvailable`, `phewMouthAvailable`) instead of `UIImage(named:)` on every frame.

**Checked and fine:** no buttons in the scene except ✕ and ⏸ (rail, ◀/▶ pair and control bar hidden); the mic stays off in Play (`PlayMic`, `talkPressed`/`micPressed`); pause stops the music, the clock and her speech (`userAction(.pauseScene)` → the voice-stall stop path) and resume works; going to the background pauses; repeats replay the game's take (`ClipStore.has` → `GameTakes`); the finale opens the clean-up by itself; the one-scene clean-up waits for ▶ The End; the relaunch resume opens paused at the saved time; schema 2–6 scenes have no `beats` and keep their path; no RealtimeSession, NutmegSpeaker or ClipStore change (voice-stall fixes hold); the music only uses `AVAudioPlayer` on the current session.
