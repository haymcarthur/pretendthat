# Grown-up font: Poppins (lighter than ExtraBold); kid screens stay as they are

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** grown-up-facing screens use **Poppins**: the marketing site, onboarding, Grown-ups, and App Store captions. Headings use Poppins **SemiBold 600** with tight letter spacing (about -0.02 to -0.03em). Haylee wanted Cal Sans's thickness with Poppins' look, and 600 is the closest stroke match to Cal Sans (side by side in design/brand-fonts/poppins-weight.html). ExtraBold 800 felt "a little too big". Body text is Poppins Regular/Medium. Kid-facing screens (stage, props, play, The End, printables) keep today's SF Rounded look, because Haylee likes how the app looks.
- **Why:** Haylee picked Poppins from the sampler (design/brand-fonts/). Different fonts for the grown-up side are fine as long as the colors, Nutmeg art, chunky buttons and logo stay shared.
- **License:** SIL Open Font License 1.1. It goes on the Code Camp team profile, and the license text ships with the app.
- **Who decided:** Haylee (Poppins, grown-up side only, lighter weight); SemiBold 600 confirmed by Haylee after the comparison.
