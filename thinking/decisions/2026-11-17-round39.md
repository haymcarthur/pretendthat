## 1. The ~10 s pause after a spoken or typed "found it" (fixed)
- **Root cause:** `RealtimeSession.awaitingReply` was set when the kid's turn ended and cleared only when her audio
  started. A "found it" turn ends with a tool-only (silent) pass: mark_found + show_next_prop, and the app says the
  next prop line itself, so no spoken follow-up is ever asked for. No audio → `awaitingReply` stayed true → `isBusy`
  stayed true → `sayAppPropLine` waited out its full 10 s guard. Card taps never set it (no kid turn), so they were fast.
- **Fix:** NutmegCore `ReplyWait` (in ResponseGate.swift) owns the flag: due on the kid's turn, cleared when her audio
  starts **or when a response ends with nothing more requested** (the gate is idle). The session calls it after every
  main-session `response.done`. A pass that does ask for her spoken reply still waits for it.
- **Test:** `Round39PauseTests` (the found-it sequence through the real `ResponseGate`: before the fix the wait stayed
  on; a spoken follow-up still waits for her audio).
- **Live check:** not run (see "Live calls" below). The fix is in the one place the flag is read, and the unit test
  replays the exact gate sequence from the spike's logs.

- The brief assumed the dark shirt shadow is in `nutmeg-writing-fingers.svg`. It isn't: that layer is only fur
  (#AD5E36) and finger creases (#9C4727), clipped to the fist. The dark shirt shadow (#470D62, 2 paths) is in
  `nutmeg-body-writing.svg`, which never rocks. What reads as "the shadow moves" is the rocking fist uncovering the
  body's own paw edge and crease underneath it (at -3.2° you see a doubled crease; at +3.2° the body's paw edge shows
  next to the shadow line). Rendered proof: /tmp/pt-round39/writing-hand-sheet.png (rock -3.2 / 0 / +3.2, the fingers
  layer alone, the body layer alone).
- No shadow paths to move, so per the rule (only if low risk, never redraw) nothing was changed. **For Haylee:** the
  real fixes are art changes: a writing body with the paw (and its shirt shadow) cut out under the fingers layer, made
  the art way (regenerate, not patch), or a smaller rock (±3.2° → ±1.5°) as a code-only stopgap.

## 3. Auto-save round (built)
- **The show counts when the kit is made.** `KitMadeLatch` (NutmegCore) fires once per new game when it reaches
  Print with a kit; `countNewShow()` moved there from Let's play! (`startConversation`). Closing before the kit costs
  nothing and saves nothing. Replays never count. The New game gate is unchanged (it still checks shows left first).
- **Every new game saves itself at that moment** (free and paid). `AppModel.autoSave` writes game content only
  (rule 6, the same `SavedGame` fields and privacy test as D1) with the clips recorded so far; from then on her new
  lines go straight into the saved game (D3b), and the save is updated (`SavedGameStore.update`, which keeps the clips
  and dates, read under the lock) when both page sets land, when the cover is drawn, when the kit or guide changes, and
  at The End. A save that would wait on the play guide retries when the guide lands.
- **No Save button, no save question.** The star and `tapSave` are gone.
- **The bookshelf moment:** after the papers land and before the print-ready line, the game's cover card (else the
  sign page, else its prop stickers) appears in the card zone, shrinks and slides onto a small bookshelf (Haylee's
  bookshelf art, no words), the shelf pops with three stars, and Nutmeg says "I put it on your bookshelf!" (same words
  every game). She is on the Print stage the whole time (iPad: the card zone right of her; iPhone: below her).
  New games only (a replay is already on the shelf).
- **The clip question:** the existing clip system would bundle the line (CommonLines + `-PTRecordCommon`), but
  recording needs a live session, which wasn't run (below). So she says it live and, like every app line, the game
  keeps that recording. Same for the new End line. Both are noted in `CommonLines` for a later recording run.
- **The End:** Play it again (green round arrow, the scenes from the top) + Home. Her line: "The end! What a show!
  Take a bow! It's on your bookshelf. Tap the arrow to play again, or the house to go home!" (new games and replays).
- **Leave dialog:** before the kit: "Leave the game?" + Leave / Keep playing (the "you'll start a new game next time"
  line is gone). After the kit (or on a replay): the same, plus the game's cover card going onto the little shelf.
- **"Sounds like a game you have":** at the yes to the recap (confirm_plan, before anything is made or counted) the app
  matches the new game's type (set_game, now kept as `gameType`) + theme against the saved titles on the device, no AI
  call (`GameMatch`). On a match she says "We already have <title> on your shelf! Want to play that one?" (live, never
  kept with the game) and two big picture buttons show: the saved game's card with a green ▶ (its replay starts in
  place, nothing counted) and yellow sparkles (make a new one: the yes stands, the roles start). Asked once per game;
  never blocks (the X still works).
- **No cap on saved games.** Grown-ups → Saved games: checkboxes always on (tap a row), a red Delete (n) bar at the
  bottom with one confirm ("Delete Pet Vet?" / "Delete 3 games?"), swipe-to-delete kept, Newest / Last played / Name,
  each row's size (and the total), a search bar by name.
- **Kids' bookshelf:** most recently played first (`lastPlayedAt`; a new game's is its save time).
- A game saved halfway replays from the start like any other; scenes never reached have no recorded lines, so she says
  them live (and keeps them).

### Claude's calls (auto-save)
- **Relay show count stays at Let's play!** The relay (code only, not deployed) issues the show ticket that the
  game's Claude and image calls carry, so it must run before the kit is written. Only the on-device count moved. When
  the relay is deployed, counting there at the kit needs a "commit" step (open question).
- **Match rule:** every game-type word must be in the saved title, and the twist must match (a shared twist word, or
  neither has one); place words (shop, café, clinic…) and little words don't count; plurals ignored. "Pizza Restaurant
  in the Jungle" ≈ "Jungle Pizza Restaurant", ≠ "Pizza Restaurant", ≠ "Airplane to the Moon". The most recently played
  close game wins.
- **Picking the saved game** switches to its replay in place (the voice session stays up), so the kids never see home.
- **Play it again** replays the scenes with the same kids and props (`store.playAgain`), no reload.
- **The bookshelf moment comes before the ready line**, so "tap the printer" stays her last word on Print.
- **Grown-ups copy:** About → "You're in charge" and the empty Saved games text now say games save themselves once
  the kit is made.
- **Demo mode saves too** (the bundled airplane game auto-saves when a demo reaches Print); `-PTAutoStart` frame strips
  don't.

## 4. Poppins on the grown-up side (built)
- Poppins Regular, Medium, SemiBold 4.004 bundled from google/fonts (ofl/poppins), registered in `UIAppFonts`, the
  license shipped as `Fonts/Poppins-OFL.txt`. `GrownUpFont` maps sizes onto Poppins with `Font.custom(_:size:relativeTo:)`
  (Dynamic Type), headings SemiBold 600 with -0.02em (`headingKerning`), body Regular / Medium.
- Used on: onboarding (v2 pages via `OB.font`, the older `OBType`), every Grown-ups screen (`GUType`), the gate,
  the plans sheet, Report a problem, and the navigation-bar titles (only grown-up screens have them). Kid screens keep
  SF Rounded (`Theme` untouched; a test checks).
