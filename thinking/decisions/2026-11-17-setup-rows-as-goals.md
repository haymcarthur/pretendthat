# Setup and clean-up rows are goals; Nutmeg's spoken line gives the specifics

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:**
  - Each setup and clean-up row is a short goal in the game's world, for example "Get the pet room ready". Nutmeg's spoken line for that row names the specific props and printed items: "...by hanging up the sign and setting up the pet bed."
  - Roughly one goal per role.
  - Every household prop and printed item is used on purpose somewhere.
- **Why:** after her first iPhone game, Haylee found the 2-player rows too granular and missing the props. The 4-player rows were closer.
- **Replaces:** round 37's rule of at most 2 items per row with labels that list the items.
- **Who decided:** Haylee.
- **Built:** round 40 ([decision](2026-11-17-round40.md)).
