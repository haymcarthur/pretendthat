## What was built

### 1. Events are scenarios (NutmegCore `EventScenarios.swift`, `OneScene.swift`)
- The guide prompt asks for a SCENARIO per event (a situation + a general direction the called roles act out for a minute or two, often with a printout), with Haylee's good examples ("A space cloud is in the way! Navigator, use the map…", "The ship is wobbly! Everyone, make everything secure!") and bad ones ("Point your hand forward and zoom the ship", "Make a silly wobbly face", "Wiggle your finger to draw a path", "Wave to everyone", "Clap your hands"). The schema's `line` description says the same.
- **Light check** (`EventScenario.isGestureOnly`, Claude's call on the lists): a line with a one-off body gesture (point, wave, wiggle, clap, nod, jump once, make a … face, smile, wink, thumbs-up, high five, stomp, salute, bow, spin around…) and no scenario verb left (help, find, fix, check, secure, use, bring, steer, serve, tell…) is replaced in `OneScene.repairEvent` by an app-written scenario event for the same role(s) (`appWritten`, so the picker prefers the guide's own events). The calm-again line is unchanged.
- **App-written events are scenarios too** (the old "show everyone your best move" / "give a thumbs-up" templates are gone): three calm and three tense templates ("…a new visitor is on the way! Get everything ready and show them around!", "…oh no, everything is getting wobbly! Make everything secure and help everyone hold on!"). Tested: none is gesture-only, all speakable.
- **The opener sets the scene:** a missing or generic opener ("Get ready!", "Pretend that the game is starting!", "let's go") becomes "Pretend that we're at the <game title>! Everyone, take your places!" in the repair; when a composed kit still has a generic one (older guide), the story's "Welcome to X" gives "Pretend that we're at X!".

### 2. No auto repeat; the card stays; hear again
- The `repeatEvent` cue is gone. **The event card stays up from its chime until the next cue** (`BeatRun.eventEnds`): its calm-again for a tense event, else the next event, the last one until the finale. The calm-again card now stays 20 s (was 12 s; Claude's call: room for its 🔊).
- **🔊 hear-again** on the event card and on the calm-again card (`PTSpeakerButton`, the checklist's speaker style on the thin `Theme.edge`): `AppModel.hearBeatAgain()` says the same line through `speak`, which plays the game's recorded take when it has one (`ClipStore`), so no new call; nothing while she's already talking.
- [one-scene-music-beats](2026-11-21-one-scene-music-beats.md) got an update line (superseded: the repeat and "no hear-again").

### 3. One owner per object (`ObjectOwners.swift`)
- For the playing count, **every printed page except the badges and every prop the kids have gets exactly one playing owner.** Props first (no role more than one prop more than another, so the clean-up stays fair), then the pages (no role more than one object more than another). Within the fair choices an object goes to the role it suits (the prop's own role, then a playing role whose guide item names it), else the least busy, earliest-ranked one. Deterministic. On the bundled game with 6 kids every object stays with the role the guide gave it.
- **Setup cards** (`OneScene.setupScene`, replaces `playerScene` + `cover` for one-scene kits): each role's card has rows for its own objects only, worded with the guide's own item when one names exactly that object, else the app's job; 1 row per object while they fit in 3 rows, then pairs ("Set up the snack cart and the carry-on bag"). Max 3 rows; **a solo kid with more than 6 objects gets more rows** (Claude's call: 1 kid with the bundled game has 9 objects, which can't fit 3 rows of 2). A role with fewer than 2 rows gets simple role items: its own guide items that name no object, then "Put on your badge", then "Get in your spot".
- **The badges are personal, outside the ownership** (Claude's call): each kid puts on their own, so the setup cue says it once to everyone: "Get the Plane Ready! Everyone, put on your badge. Tap your picture to hear your jobs." (`OneScene.badgeCue`). The guide prompt no longer asks for badge items and says every page has ONE owner.
- **`OneSceneCoverage` is "exactly once"** (`counts`, `missing`, `repeated`, `exactlyOnce`), tested at counts 1–6 with all props, a skipped prop, extra props and none, plus the **hidden roles 5–6 case** (jobs written for R5/R6 and a duplicate of the menu on R1 fold into R1–R4's cards once).
- **Clean-up uses the same owners:** each prop is put away by its setup owner, then the one print-mess item goes to a least busy role (still fair, max difference 1, tested).

### 4. Prop stand-ins (`PropStandIns.swift`, review file `design/prop-stand-ins.md`)
- **Every library prop has 10–15 believable pretend things it can become** (Haylee's update during the round: a big range across theme families: space, jungle/farm/dinosaurs, castle/magic, shop/restaurant/doctor/school, sea, wild west, superheroes, pirates, rescue, spooky, snowy north, robots/lab, circus, sports). Judged by shape and function for a 4–8 year old (sock → sock puppet, snake, tail, worm, caterpillar, elephant trunk…; ball → planet, moon, snowball, egg, pumpkin…; laundry basket → boat, cage, cockpit, shopping cart, crib…; never a sock planet). It includes every curated use in `PropLibrary.uses` (tested). No family tags yet (optional; not done).
- **Fuzzy match** (`match` / `allows`): same head noun, or a compound's last word ("sock puppet" ↔ "puppet"); filler words (big, magic, pretend…) ignored.
- **The weave prompt** lists each prop's stand-ins as its ONLY allowed pretend uses and asks for the pretend name only.
- **Validation, no AI call:**
  - In the hunt (`AdventureStore` `.showNextProp`, after the existing fit/printed/shortlist checks): a pair off the prop's list is settled by `PropStandIns.settle`: a library prop whose stand-ins include the use (unused, on the shortlist, fits) takes its place with the same use (the game clearly needs that thing: sock → planet becomes a ball → planet); else the prop becomes its own closest stand-in (same `PropFit` need, else a shared word, else its first). The tool output tells the voice model ("use THIS item and use"), like the other app swaps.
  - In the weave and the setup cards (`PropStandIns.itemText`): an item that turns the prop into another prop's stand-in ("Hold up the planet" for a sock puppet) gets the prop's own pretend name.
- **Review file:** `design/prop-stand-ins.md`, generated from the Swift table by `tools/prop_stand_ins.py`; a test compares it to `PropStandIns.markdown()` where the design folder is next to the package (on the Mac; skipped in the Linux copy).

### 10b. SAFETY RULE: everything in the play world is pretend (Haylee)
- **Why:** a mockup line read "Put the pot on the stove spot"; a kid could take that as the REAL stove.
- **The rule:** no line shown or spoken (setup, clean-up, events, calm-again, opener, finale; AI-written or app-written) may direct kids to a real hazard or appliance. Everything is pretend and made from the props and printouts.
- (a) **Prompts:** the guide's rules and the weave prompt say everything is pretend, an appliance or place is the pretend one tied to a prop or page ("the pretend stove", "the play oven page"), and never to send kids to a real stove, oven, microwave, toaster, sink, bath, water tap, outlet, cord, fireplace, candle, window, stairs, the car, outside, medicine, cleaning products or knives, or to climb.
- (b) **The check (`Speakable.safe`, part of `Speakable.repair`):** appliance / hazard words without "pretend", "play", "toy", "paper", "cardboard" or "printed" in front get "pretend" ("the stove spot" → "the pretend stove spot"; stove, oven, cooktop, microwave, toaster, fireplace, campfire, heater, grill, kitchen/bathroom sink, bathtub, bath, shower, window, plug, cord, medicine, pills, bleach, cleaner, fire; "fire truck", "firefighter" and friends stay). A line that SENDS kids to a real hazard (climb…, stairs, outside/outdoors, street, balcony, the pool, faucet, water tap, turn on the water, outlet, socket, counter top, to the sink, open the window, get in the car, "the real") is replaced: an event by the app's scenario event for its roles, the opener by the scene-setting opener, the finale by "We did it! Everyone, celebrate!", a calm-again by a calm-again line, a setup item by "Get your spot ready", a clean-up item by "Tidy up your spot". Claude's call on both lists.
- **One text, stored:** the repair runs on the STORED guide (`GuideRepair.repair` → `Speakable.repair(scene)`) on every guide that lands and on every saved game that loads (replays, older schemas too), and again on the composed rows; the card, her voice and the saved game use the same words (no separate spoken version; tested: the setup row's spoken text is exactly "Role, " + the shown text, and a second pass changes nothing).
- Stand-ins were checked too ("stairs", "street sweeper" and the candle uses were replaced; tested: no stand-in needs a swap or is a hazard).
- (c)/(d) Tests: the words and the "stove spot" case, the hazard directions, events/items rewritten in a stored guide, an older checklist guide made safe on load, every app template safe.

### 11. Music on/off (Haylee)

### 12. ⏸ Pause and the music (verify only, Haylee wasn't sure it was a bug)
- **Read through, no gap found, nothing changed:** `pauseScene` calls `music.pause()`, which sets `paused`, cancels the fade tasks and pauses every playing player and the chime. While paused: `beatTick` doesn't call `music.play` (it's inside `if !scenePaused`); `play`, `playChime` and `setDucked` all return early on `paused`, so the review pass's restart-on-tick (inside `play`) can't bring a loop back; a fade task only ever pauses. Resume: `music.resume()` restarts the segment that was playing (a finished win ending isn't replayed), and a relaunch-resumed scene (opens paused without `music.pause()`) starts its segment on the first tick after ▶ Resume. End scene while paused un-pauses first.

### 13. Nutmeg's face drifting during a zoom (root cause and fix)
- **Root cause (two parts):**
  1. The stage camera's implicit `.animation(Theme.stageMove, value: f)` (`PTStageView`, PTConversation.swift) sits around the whole stage, so it reached INTO the rig: when the frame changed in the same update as her state (an event card coming up with the alarmed face, the calm-again, the finale, setup → scene), the head's pose offset, rotation and scale and the body's transform each picked up the camera's spring, layer by layer.
  2. In `NutmegCharacter`, the head's `.offset` and `.rotationEffect` mixed the pose's layout values with per-frame motion (breathing follow, the nod, the talking tilt). The `TimelineView` re-renders 30 times a second with no animation, so every tick re-set those modifiers un-animated: the head jumped to its end place while the body (whose transform didn't change between ticks) kept easing. Face, eyes and mouth are head layers, so they slid off the body/head for a moment.
- **Fix:** the camera moves her as ONE piece: the zoom/move stays one scale + position on the container (unchanged), and the rig's subtree is cut from the camera transaction (`.transaction(value: f) { $0.animation = nil }` on the character, before the container's scale/position). In the rig, the per-frame motion (talk tilt, breathing follow, nod) is its own modifier group with `.transaction { $0.animation = nil }`, and the pose's layout values (head scale, rotation, offset) are separate modifiers that only change at a pose swap, so they ease in the same transaction as the body swap. Layer swaps (face, mouth, blink) were already instant. The intentional state tilt keeps its own scoped ease. The peek (`NutmegPeek`) draws its head in one `.position` from fixed numbers and isn't moved by the camera, so it's unchanged.
- **Check on the device:** setup → ▶ Let's play! (zoom into the scene), an event (prop-card framing + talking/alarmed face), its calm-again (phew), the finale (yay + celebrate), back to the clean-up cards; plus a pose swap (celebrate) while talking.
