# Auto-save every new game; count a show only once the kit is made

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:**
  - A new show counts toward the Free Preview or the plan's month only when Nutmeg makes the printable kit (end of Props), not at "Let's play!". Closing before that costs nothing.
  - Every new game, free or paid, saves itself automatically at that same moment, so a game is on the bookshelf even if they quit halfway or the app is swiped away.
- **Why:** kids who closed early or skipped the save button lost one of their 2 free games and had nothing to replay. One rule for everyone, so families never wonder why some games saved and others didn't.
- **Experience:** approved by Haylee 2026-11-17; plus the "sounds like a game you have" check and no cap on saved games (queue-next-app-round.md, "Auto-save round").
- **Known wrinkle:** a game saved halfway has recorded lines only for the scenes they played; on a replay Nutmeg speaks the rest live.
- **Who decided:** Haylee (auto-save for everyone, don't count early closes); moving the count to the kit is Claude's call.
