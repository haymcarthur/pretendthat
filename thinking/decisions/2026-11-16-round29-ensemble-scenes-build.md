# Round 29 build: ensemble scenes (2026-11-16)

Builds Haylee's [ensemble scenes](2026-11-16-ensemble-scenes.md) design. Who decided: Haylee (the design); the implementation calls below are Claude's.

## What changed
- **Guide schema (NutmegCore):** 3 scenes (`KitScenesSix.count = 3`, `GuideRepair.minScenes = 3`, `Sanitizer.Limits.scenesMax = 3`). Each scene is `title`, `nutmegSays` (the "Pretend that…" setup) and ONE task string per role `R1`…`R6` plus `solo`: 21 short strings, required fields. No pictures, no scene ids, no top-level line in the schema (they were output tokens nothing showed). Tasks are 1–3 minute, one-sentence (≤ two parts) interactive jobs that connect across roles, use only real props / pretend stand-ins / kit items, and may imply talking but never script lines.
- **Solo (Claude's call):** a separate `solo` task per scene, written as "a slightly bigger version of R1's task that needs no one else". Chosen over merging R1+R2 (a merged task reads as two jobs for one kid and breaks the "≤ two parts" rule) and over a separate call (cost). It adds 3 short strings (~+15% guide output). One kid sees the solo task as R1's row; a guide with no solo falls back to R1's task. Solo text is not "unnamed" (it may talk about pretend customers, which would otherwise match a role title).
- **Visible rows:** `PlayCast.rows(scene, roles, players)` = one row per playing role in rank order (solo: one row). `PlayCast.kit` keeps the 3 scenes (no hand-off turns). A count change still makes no AI call.
- **GuideRepair:** placeholder titles, a short guide padded to 3 template scenes (a task per role from its job + a solo task), **no scripted lines** (`unscripted`: a speaking verb with quoted words loses the quote; if nothing is left it becomes "say it in your own words"; other quote marks are dropped, words kept), the props woven in, then round 28's real-props check. A missing prop goes to R1's task in any scene first, then R2's, then its own role's (so it shows at low counts), plus that scene's solo task when free; a one-part task gets "with the <prop>", a two-part task is rewritten around the prop. Idempotent.
- **Per-prop weave (Claude call during the hunt):** picks 1–2 role tasks plus the solo task of one of those scenes (`maxUsesPerProp = 3`, `maxWords = 26`), keeps the interaction, no quotes.
- **Play UI:** no hand-off card, no per-role rotation, no checkboxes, no Done, no auto-advance. The scene card: title, a row per playing role (`PTTaskRow`: the role's character picture, the task, a 56 pt 🔊), and one big green `PTNextSceneButton` (▶ + "Next scene") under the card; after scene 3 it goes to The End. The round 14 sizing rules stay (measured tiers; 6 long rows fit on the 13" stage; if they ever don't, the rows scroll and the ▶ stays visible). Chat open: the card (rows + ▶) is in the panel's tab, now labelled **Scene** (theatre masks) instead of Checklist.
- **Character picture (Claude's call):** `BadgeCharacter.rect` scans the badge slice's middle strip for the two blank banners the badge prompt asks for and keeps the art between them, trimming 11% each side for the frame; banners not found → the prompt's layout (26%–80%). Shown in a circle with a role-color ring; the role color chip (first letter) until the color badge sheet is drawn.
- **Voice:** a scene opens with ONE flowing line: setup + each playing role's task (each starts with the role's title); the first scene adds "Tap the green play button for the next scene!". Any tap (▶, 🔊, Repeat, rail) stops her first (round 19 barge-in). 🔊 reads just that row (`HandOffLines.task`), straight to the voice so it can be heard twice. Repeat (pill) re-reads the whole scene and, during a scene, is never dimmed by her speaking (it's a tap that cuts in). Voice "next scene" / "we're done" → new tool `next_scene` (the app says the next scene; she says nothing). Removed tools: `confirm_handoff`, `finish_scene`. The checklist cheers (`ChecklistCheers`) and hand-off lines are gone; the bundled "Here's your checklist again!" clip was deleted.
- **Clips:** `ClipRecorder.maxSeconds` 30 → 90 s, so a 6-task scene opening (~40–60 s) can be recorded and replayed in her real voice. Task read-outs are recorded per row the first time (D3b live once, then saved).
- **Navigation:** the rail's Play ↺ at The End reopens the last scene and says it again (tap and voice `go_to_step play`).
- **Saved games:** schema 2; v1 games (round 28's hand-off guide) are refused by `SavedGame.decode` and stay off the shelf (the app isn't live). Replays play the same 3 scenes with the replay's prop answers (skips → "pretend …" in role and solo tasks).
- **Bundled airplane game** rewritten to the new shape (3 scenes × 6 interacting tasks + solo).
- **Tools:** `tools/live-voice.sh` takes `DD=<derived data path>` (default unchanged). DemoDriver: `#next`, `#task N`, `#scene`, `-PTPlayers N`, `-PTDemo scene|scene2|sceneLong`.
