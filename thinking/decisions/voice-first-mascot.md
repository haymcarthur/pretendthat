# Voice-first mascot

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** A chat-style experience with voice. A character talks with the kid and walks them through one step at a time: plan, then props and setup, then printables, then play.

**Why:** Non-readers. The character is the main interaction point and must be very polished (aesthetics bar: last year's 1st-place Dominican Republic Dice).
