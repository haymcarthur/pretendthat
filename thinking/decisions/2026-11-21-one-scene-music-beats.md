# One scene with music and Nutmeg's beats (Haylee, 2026-11-21: idea, not decided yet)

**The idea (Haylee and her husband):** a setup checklist, then ONE long play scene. Kids press play when they're set up. Background music plays while they play. At set moments Nutmeg chimes in with a role-specific event ("Oh no, a red light is going off! Engineer, go check it out", "Debris ahead! Captain, steer around it"), 2–3 tasks during the scene. The scene has beats (calm, tense/urgent, relief, happy) and the music follows them. Then a clean-up checklist.

**Music options she raised:** a collection of theme songs (space, jungle, desert, magical, diner…) vs one song with the beats built in, so kids learn the cue for "something's coming" (like Stardew Valley's music by area).

**Claude's take (2026-11-21):**
- Good direction: longer free play, fewer screen taps, and Nutmeg becomes a real game master. Reuses the setup/clean-up checklist UI, the voice fix, resume and the buttons from the 11-20 rounds. Replaces the per-scene checklists + scenarios.
- Music: themed tracks, each made of the same beat SEGMENTS (calm loop, tense loop, relief/win sting, short "event" chime). The app loops calm as long as it wants and switches at an event, so the music follows the game, not a fixed timeline. The same chime before every event teaches kids "Nutmeg's about to say something". Start with a small set of theme families; the guide picks a music tag from a fixed list.
- Watch-outs: duck the music + chime before she speaks (kids are loud); every role must get an event (scale events with player count or name 2 roles per event); events don't need a tap to finish; an optional pause/"more time" button; licensing for commercial music; app size.

## Decided (Haylee, 2026-11-21)

- **The scene is about 10 minutes.**

## Claude's calls (defaults; change after a family test)
- Flow: setup checklist (per-role cards, check + 🔊) → ▶ Play → short "Pretend that…" opener → ~10 min of music with events → finale for everyone → clean-up checklist → The End.
- Events: every playing role is named at least once. About 3 events for 1–2 kids, 4 for 3–4, 5 for 5–6 (an event can call two roles), plus the finale. Spaced ~1.5–3 min apart. Beats like calm → calm event → tense event → relief → calm event → tense event → win finale.
- Every event: chime, music ducks, Nutmeg names the role first, then one short thing to do. No tap to finish. Grown-up controls: pause and "more time".
- Music: themed tracks from a fixed list of families, each made of the same segments (calm loop, tense loop, relief/win ending, shared event chime). The guide picks the family.
- **Update (Haylee):** no button pressing at all once play begins. The iPad runs in the background, kids stay away from it and follow Nutmeg ("the director") as things happen. No pause, more-time or hear-again buttons during play (only the existing small grown-up ✕). Claude's calls: Nutmeg repeats each event once by herself (~15 s later); event cards are display-only and readable from across the room; the finale flows into the clean-up checklist by itself. Buttons exist only on the setup and clean-up checklists.
- **Update (Haylee):** a ⏸ pause button during play is fine (kids may want to pause). What she meant by "no buttons": nothing to confirm a task was done. Events just play.
- **Resolution beats (Haylee):** a problem event is later resolved by Nutmeg saying things are calm again, never by claiming the kids fixed it. Example: "There's a big lunch rush! Quick, waiter, take the new people to their seats, and cook, get ready to make more food!" … later … "Phew, the rush has died down." It works whether or not they acted it out.
- **Fair calls (Haylee):** an event can call more than one role, but no role gets left out: kids notice if the others are called 2–3 times and they're called once. Rule: each playing role is called a near-equal number of times (max difference 1), counted in code.
- **Mockup choices (Haylee, design/mockups/one-scene/):** show the music pill and the ~10 min scene bar; hide the step rail during play; Pause is a labeled pill; the calm-again line shows in a soft card; two-role events show both pictures and names. No queued/NEXT marking on checklist rows (the iPhone setup mockup's dashed row is wrong).
- **Expressions (Haylee):** Nutmeg's face changes with what's going on. Claude's mapping from existing faces (happy, talking, listening, thinking, ooh, yay, oops, nap, focus; ears normal/perked/flop): calm play = happy, gently bobbing to the music; calm event = talking/happy + perked ears; tense event = ooh + perked ears; calm-again = happy (eyes soft); finale = yay + the celebrate pose. Missing art to generate (Haylee, ChatGPT → Figma): an **alarmed/urgent** face ("uh-oh!", worried brows) for tense events and a **relieved "phew"** face for the calm-again lines. The code uses them when the assets exist, falling back to ooh/happy (the same pattern as `focusAvailable`).
- **Clean-up checklist (Haylee):** one item for EVERY prop the kids got out, plus one item to clean up the print mess (paper scraps, crayons, scissors). Spread fairly across the playing roles, enforced in code.
