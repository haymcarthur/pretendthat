# D4: Camera "zoom" on Nutmeg

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Haylee

**Decision:** While Nutmeg is talking or listening, the frame smoothly zooms toward her face. When she presents things (game ideas, roles, props), it zooms out and the items appear arranged around her.

**Why:** Feels like a real conversation (close-up) and a show-and-tell (wide shot); motion carries the flow without text.
