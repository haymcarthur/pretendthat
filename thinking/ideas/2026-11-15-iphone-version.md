# iPhone version (mockup stage, 2026-11-15)

Status: **built 2026-11-17** ([decisions/2026-11-17-iphone-build.md](../decisions/2026-11-17-iphone-build.md)). Mockups are in design/mockups/iphone/.
Haylee's feedback on the first mockups:
- **No pointing on the phone:** Nutmeg holds her clipboard and looks at it (the existing clipboard/reading pose) when a card is below her. No new pointing-down art is needed.
- **Empty lower area:** Claude's suggestion: when no card or choice is showing, the stage camera grows to fill the space (Nutmeg bigger, as on the iPad close-ups), plus optionally a caption bubble of her current line.
- **The chat sheet covers her completely** when open (full-height sheet); the control pill stays on top.
- **No mute/quiet mode:** most steps (the prop hunt, play) need voice anyway, and the device volume covers quiet places. Revisit only if users ask.
