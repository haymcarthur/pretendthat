- **Today's cost of free replays** (research/2026-11-16-cost-per-game-v2.md, roundD3b):
  - first replay of a saved game: about 14¢ (a few unrecorded lines plus live replies);
  - later replays: about 3¢;
  - a fully recorded game: about $0.
  - A cancelled family replaying daily costs roughly $1 a month.
  - Games auto-saved halfway can cost ~40–50¢ on their first replay, because the unplayed scenes are said live once, then recorded.
- **The option:** with no active plan, replays still play fully with every recorded line, but Nutmeg holds no live conversation (no Realtime session; kids use the buttons, the mic is off). The cost per replay becomes about $0, and nothing is taken away from their saved games.
- **Trigger to add it:** analytics or the relay show replay voice spend from non-subscribers becoming meaningful (e.g. >10% of monthly AI spend).
- **Keep the promise:** saved games always stay playable and free.
