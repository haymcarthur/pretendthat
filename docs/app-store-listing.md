## Description (4000 max)
Turn your living room into a pretend-play adventure.

Meet Nutmeg, a friendly mouse stage manager for kids ages 4 to 8. Kids tell her what they want to play, like "a bakery on the moon" or "a vet clinic for sea animals," and she helps them plan the game, find props at home and print a kit. Then she steps back, and the play happens off the screen.

PICK A GAME BY TALKING
No reading or typing needed. Kids talk with Nutmeg, and she offers ideas, asks how many kids are playing (1 to 6) and gives everyone a role. Big picture buttons let kids who can't read yet lead the game. Nutmeg never asks for names; she uses roles like "the Captain" or "the Head Baker."

FIND PROPS AT HOME
Nutmeg sends kids on a prop hunt, one thing at a time: a bowl becomes a mixing cauldron, a blanket becomes a stage curtain. Only soft, safe household things, and no toys to buy. Don't have something? She swaps it.

PRINT A CUSTOM KIT
Every new game gets its own printable kit, drawn just for it: role badges, a sign, play money, tickets, a menu and more. Print in full color, or as coloring pages kids color in themselves, with AirPrint.

PLAY, SCENE BY SCENE
Nutmeg tells a short story, then guides the setup, three play scenes and the clean-up. Each player gets their own part, which they can tap to hear again.

EVERY GAME SAVES ITSELF
Each new game lands on Nutmeg's bookshelf with its own picture, ready to play again with a different number of players. Saved games sync through your family's own iCloud, so they show up on your other iPads and iPhones.

MADE FOR FAMILIES

• Nutmeg is an AI helper. A grown-up agrees to the AI partners before kids play

- Notes:

PretendThat is a voice-first pretend-play helper for kids 4–8. No account is needed.

To try it: finish the short grown-up setup (agree to the AI partners), then hold the microphone button and say what to play, e.g. "a bakery". Typing works too: tap the keyboard button. Nutmeg plans the game, sends kids on a prop hunt, makes a printable kit (you can view it without printing), and guides the scenes.

The Free Preview includes 2 new games, so a reviewer can play a full game without buying. The Show Pass and Season Pass subscriptions are in the Grown-ups area (behind a grown-ups check) and on the paywall after the free games.

AI: Nutmeg's replies and voice come from OpenAI; the story and play guide come from Anthropic (Claude). Kids' audio never leaves the device (Apple on-device speech recognition); only text, with names removed, is sent through our relay. Content is filtered for kid safety on the device and by the AI providers.
